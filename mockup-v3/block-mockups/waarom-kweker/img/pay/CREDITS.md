# Payment marks

- `ideal.svg`, `visa.svg`, `mastercard.svg`, `maestro.svg`, `vpay.svg`: the payment marks in card frames, from the
  open-source collection activemerchant/payment_icons (https://github.com/activemerchant/payment_icons, MIT licence,
  Copyright 2015 Shopify Inc). iDEAL, Visa, Mastercard, Maestro and V PAY are trademarks of their owners; shops that accept
  these methods show their marks. The other payment icons in the footer are drawn for this project.
- Which methods the nursery accepts is provisional (owner, 22 Nov: online iDEAL and Visa / Mastercard; on receipt debit
  cards Maestro / V PAY and Visa / Mastercard, or cash; businesses on invoice; to be confirmed by the company owner).
