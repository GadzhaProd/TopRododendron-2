## 1. Do they offer a growth guarantee, what exactly is promised, and for how long?

### Takeaway
Only a minority of rhododendron specialists publish a real consumer growth guarantee. Most German growers' AGB explicitly say **no** Anwachsgarantie unless agreed separately (sometimes for an extra fee, then 1 year). Among the Dutch specialist webshops, Rhododendronwal promises "3 maanden aangroei-garantie" with replacement or refund, and Park Graveland advertises "Altijd met groeigarantie" without published terms. Esveld (Boskoop) refers to a 6-month legal guarantee and says nobody can give a 100% guarantee. The most consumer-friendly wording found comes from a generalist nursery (Garten-Schlüter): 6 months, free replacement or refund at the customer's choice.

### Inferences
- Among real specialists there are three models: (a) **no guarantee, legal warranty only** (Hachmann, Böhlje, Plantenwelt, Rhodo-online); (b) **guarantee only on request, for a fee, 1 year, aimed at trade/landscaping buyers** (Heinje, Bruns, both using near-identical wording that looks like a common industry AGB template); (c) **a short consumer guarantee used as a selling point** (Rhododendronwal 3 months; Park Graveland unspecified; Azaleashop 4 weeks of bloom).
- In the Dutch consumer market for large root-ball rhododendrons, the published benchmark for the nearest competitor is **3 months, replacement or refund, conditional on following planting instructions**. A guarantee of 6 months to 1 year with clear terms would stand out against these specialists. Generalist web nurseries already advertise 6–12 months.
- Esveld's "6 maanden" refers to the legal position. In my understanding, Dutch consumer law since 2022 presumes a defect that appears within **1 year** was present at delivery, so Esveld's wording may be out of date. This is my legal reading and was not verified in this session; get it checked before relying on it.
- Inconsistencies on competitor sites (Rhododendronwal's 30 days vs 3 months; "gratis" vs €75 delivery) show that a single consistent guarantee statement across header, product page, guarantee page and terms is itself a quality signal.

## 2. Conditions and exclusions specific to rhododendrons (soil/pH, planting, watering, timing, frost/drought, sun)

### Takeaway
No specialist found ties its guarantee to buying its own branded rhododendron soil. The conditions are: planting "according to instructions" (Rhododendronwal, with an instructions page stressing acidic humus-rich soil, correct depth and watering) and correct planting depth, fertilising and watering (Bruns). Exclusions are winter/late-frost and extreme weather damage, unsuitable soil type, too much or too little water (Esveld), and drought, frost and pests as force majeure (Bruns, Heinje). Hachmann shapes the risk through seasonal shipping windows instead.

### Cited Findings
- Rhododendronwal ties the guarantee to its planting instructions: "mits deze op de voorschriften geplant zijn. Zie hiervoor Planten en verplanten van Rhododendrons." [provided they are planted according to instructions; see "Planting and transplanting rhododendrons".] — [Rhododendronwal, Garantie](https://www.rhododendronwal.nl/garantie)
- The linked instructions page sets out the rhododendron conditions in effect:
  - "Vooral de juiste standplaats, zure bodem, plantdiepte en voldoende water zijn belangrijk." [Above all, the right site, acidic soil, planting depth and enough water are important.]
  - "Een rhododendron groeit het best in een zure, humusrijke en goed doorlatende bodem." [A rhododendron grows best in acidic, humus-rich, well-drained soil.]
  - "Is de bestaande grond niet geschikt, dan kun je deze verbeteren met tuinturf of geschikte grond voor zuurminnende planten." [If the existing soil is unsuitable, improve it with garden peat or soil for acid-loving plants.]
  - "Rhododendrons uit een pot kunnen gedurende een groot deel van het jaar worden geplant. Vermijd wel periodes met vorst, extreme hitte of langdurige droogte." [Pot-grown rhododendrons can be planted for much of the year, but avoid periods of frost, extreme heat or prolonged drought.]
  - "Plant een rhododendron niet te diep. De bovenkant van de kluit kan ongeveer gelijk liggen met het omliggende grondoppervlak." [Do not plant too deep; the top of the root ball should be roughly level with the surrounding soil.]
  - "Houd de bodem daarom tijdens de eerste periode licht vochtig." [Keep the soil lightly moist during the first period.]
  — [Rhododendronwal, Rhododendron planten](https://www.rhododendronwal.nl/rhododendron/rhododendron-planten)
- Rhododendronwal's product-page FAQ: "Rhododendrons gedijen het beste in zure, goed doorlatende grond en een halfschaduwrijke standplaats ... Het is aan te raden om jaarlijks in het voorjaar te bemesten met een speciale rhododendronmeststof." [Rhododendrons thrive best in acidic, well-drained soil and partial shade ... annual spring feeding with a special rhododendron fertiliser is recommended.] — [Rhododendronwal product page](https://www.rhododendronwal.nl/product/rhododendron-cosmopolitan-rood-roze)
- Esveld's exclusions: "Schadevergoeding voor winterschade of schade door late nachtvorst of andere extreme weersomstandigheden wordt in geen geval gegeven. Ook schade veroorzaakt door onjuiste behandeling zoals bijvoorbeeld het planten in voor de betreffende plantensoort ongeschikte grondtypes, maar ook te veel of te weinig water, kan niet worden vergoed." Excluded: "Fälle höherer Gewalt, insbesondere Dürre, Frost, Schädlingsbefall" [force majeure, especially drought, frost, pest infestation]. — [Bruns AGB](https://www.bruns.de/agb/)
- Heinje: the guarantee requires "dass Transport, Pflanzung und Pflege einwandfrei waren" [that transport, planting and care were faultless]. Elsewhere: "Frost, Dürre, Mangel an Transportmitteln und Streik werden höherer Gewalt gleichgestellt." [Frost, drought, lack of transport and strikes are treated as force majeure.] — [Heinje AGB](https://www.heinje.de/agb/)
- Hachmann's retention-of-title clause defines expected care: "Hierzu gehören insbesondere richtige Lagerung, Pflanzung, Düngung und/oder Bewässerung." [This includes in particular correct storage, planting, fertilising and/or watering.] — [Hachmann AGB](https://www.hachmann.de/agb/)
- Hachmann's seasonal shipping window: "Der Versand mit DHL erfolgt innerhalb von sieben Werktagen während der Saisons im Frühjahr zwischen dem 10. März und 10. Mai sowie im Herbst zwischen dem 10. Bei Bestellungen außerhalb der Saison erfolgt der Versand zu Beginn der nächsten Saison" [DHL dispatch within seven working days in spring (10 March–10 May) and autumn (10 Sept–20 Nov); orders outside these windows ship at the start of the next season]. — [Hachmann, Versand und Zahlung](https://www.hachmann.de/versand-und-zahlung/)
- Garten-Schlüter (benchmark): forgives minor care errors but excludes obvious misuse: "Solange die Pflanze nicht aus offensichtlich unsachgemäßen Gründen eingeht (z. B. Pflanzung in Sand statt Erde oder völlig unsachgemäßer Rückschnitt), greifen wir Dir unter die Arme" [As long as the plant does not die for obviously improper reasons (e.g. planting in sand instead of soil, or completely wrong pruning), we help you out]. Also: "alle leichten und versehentliche Pflegefehler sind mit abgedeckt" [all minor and accidental care mistakes are covered]. The guarantee applies to pot plants only: "Saatgut, Wurzelware und andere Lieferformen sind davon ausgenommen" [seed, bare-root and other forms are excluded]. — [Garten-Schlüter Anwachsgarantie](https://www.garten-schlueter.de/ueber-uns/anwachsgarantie/)

### Inferences
- The rhododendron-specific condition that recurs is **soil type** (acidic, humus-rich; "ongeschikte grondtypes" excluded), followed by **planting depth** and **watering**. Weather (frost, late frost, drought) is excluded by every specialist that publishes conditions.
- No specialist requires proof of using a specific branded rhododendron soil. Rhododendronwal achieves a similar effect by referring to its instructions page. An Otterlo nursery could link its guarantee to a short planting checklist (pH/soil, depth, watering), possibly with a recommended soil, without making a branded product purchase compulsory.
- Hachmann's fixed shipping windows reduce failures from planting in hot or frozen periods. A nursery with its own transport and root-ball stock could do the same by stating guarantee-valid planting periods, or by keeping the guarantee all-year (as Rhododendronwal claims) as a differentiator.

### Gaps
- No specialist was found that states a pH threshold (for example "pH < 5.5") in its guarantee conditions, or that ties the guarantee to sun-scorch exclusions specifically.
- Park Graveland's guarantee conditions are unknown (see section 1).

## 3. How to claim (photos, order number, deadline, plant returned?, who pays replacement delivery?)

### Takeaway
Published claim procedures are short. The common elements are written notice or email, photos and order/invoice number, and fixed deadlines: 48 hours for arrival damage (Park Graveland); 30 days for quality complaints (Esveld); 14 days for obvious defects (Hachmann); 5–8 days and at most 1 year (Heinje/Bruns). Return of the dead plant is generally not requested for growth claims, except Esveld asking for wrong-variety plants back. Who pays replacement delivery is rarely stated. Garten-Schlüter refunds instead of re-shipping abroad, and Hachmann bears shipping costs for its own partial deliveries.

### Cited Findings
- Park Graveland, arrival damage:
  - "Meld het binnen 48 uur: Neem binnen 48 uur na bezorging contact met ons op via e-mail." [Report it within 48 hours: contact us by email within 48 hours of delivery.]

- Garten-Schlüter (benchmark):

[Use the contact form, choose "guarantee case", attach a photo and say whether you want replacement or refund.]
  - "Die Ersatzlieferung ist in der Regel nach wenigen Tagen bei dir" [the replacement usually arrives within a few days].
  — [Garten-Schlüter Anwachsgarantie](https://www.garten-schlueter.de/ueber-uns/anwachsgarantie/)
- Hachmann: "Der Kunde ist verpflichtet, uns offensichtliche Mängel an der gelieferten Ware innerhalb von 14 Tagen nach Erhalt der Ware anzuzeigen. Andernfalls ist ein Berufen auf solche offensichtlichen Mängel ausgeschlossen." [The customer must notify obvious defects within 14 days of receipt; otherwise reliance on them is excluded.] Also: "Zur Erhaltung der Rechte ... genügt die rechtzeitige Absendung der Anzeige." [Sending the notice in time is sufficient.] — [Hachmann AGB](https://www.hachmann.de/agb/)
- Heinje has two deadlines in the same AGB: "Hierbei festgestellte Mängel sind unverzüglich - spätestens jedoch binnen acht Tagen nach Kenntnis - schriftlich zu rügen. ### Inferences
- A clear claim process is a recognisable "premium" pattern: early contact so the nursery can give care advice, then photo plus order number via a dedicated form or email, then the customer chooses replacement or refund. Specialists mostly leave this unwritten.
- No specialist asks for the dead plant to be returned for a growth claim. Photos are the standard evidence. For root-ball plants of 40–120 cm, photos (plus an optional visit, since the Otterlo nursery has its own transport) are the practical route.
- Who pays for delivering the replacement is a real gap in competitors' wording. Stating it explicitly (for example "vervangende plant wordt gratis bezorgd en geplaatst" [replacement plant delivered and placed free of charge]) would be a differentiator. This is a suggestion, not something a competitor was found to do.

### Gaps
- No specialist states who pays for delivering a replacement under a growth claim, except Garten-Schlüter's "refund only abroad" rule.
- Forum or review evidence on how claims are handled in practice was not researched in this session; that would need Trustpilot, Webwinkelkeur or Kiyoh review pages.

## 5. Transport damage and returns (withdrawal right) for live plants

### Takeaway
Practice splits on the 14-day withdrawal right. Several sellers **exclude** live plants as perishable (Park Graveland citing art. 6:230p sub f BW; Azaleashop; Plantenwelt; Rhododendronwal's terms). Rhodo-online and Rhododendronwal's guarantee page **allow** returns of pot/root-ball plants at the customer's cost. For transport damage, consumer-facing shops ask for immediate notification, with 48 hours and photos at Park Graveland. Azaleashop explicitly accepts liability for damage in its own deliveries.

### Cited Findings
- Park Graveland, returns excluded:
  - "Uitzondering herroepingsrecht: Retourneren is niet mogelijk, omdat planten levend materiaal zijn." [Exception to withdrawal right: returns are not possible because plants are living material.]
  - "Conform artikel 6:230p sub f van het Burgerlijk Wetboek zijn levende planten uitgesloten van het wettelijke herroepingsrecht (de bedenktermijn van 14 dagen). Planten zijn aan bederf onderhevig ... Een retourtransport brengt onomkeerbare schade toe aan het gewas." [Under art. 6:230p(f) of the Dutch Civil Code live plants are excluded from the 14-day withdrawal right. Plants are perishable ... return transport causes irreversible damage.]
  - "Hoewel retourneren niet mogelijk is, staan wij volledig in voor de levering van gezonde, vitale planten van kwekerskwaliteit." [Although returns are not possible, we fully stand behind delivering healthy, vital, nursery-quality plants.]
  — [Park Graveland, Verzend- en retourbeleid](https://parkgraveland.nl/verzend-en-retourbeleid/)
- Park Graveland's shipping practice: "Verwerkingstijd: Uw bestelling wordt binnen 3 dagen verzonden." [Dispatched within 3 days.] "Om te voorkomen dat planten onnodig een weekend overstaan in een distributiecentrum, verzenden wij bestellingen uitsluitend op werkdagen (maandag tot en met donderdag)." [To avoid plants sitting in a depot over the weekend, we ship only Monday to Thursday.] Packaging keeps "wortels en takken tijdens het transport vochtig en onbeschadigd" [roots and branches moist and undamaged in transit]. — [Park Graveland, Verzend- en retourbeleid](https://parkgraveland.nl/verzend-en-retourbeleid/)
- Rhododendronwal, contradictory statements:
  - The guarantee page allows withdrawal: "U heeft het recht gedurende 14 dagen na ontvangst, uw bestelling zonder opgave van redenen te ontbinden en de geleverde producten te retourneren, mits ongebruikt/ onveranderd ... De kosten voor terugzending komen voor rekening van de klant." [You may cancel within 14 days of receipt and return the products, provided unused/unchanged ... return costs are borne by the customer.] — [Rhododendronwal, Garantie](https://www.rhododendronwal.nl/garantie)
  - The general terms list as excludable: "Producten die snel bederven of een beperkte houdbaarheid hebben; (oa tuinplanten/ bomen)." [Products that spoil quickly or have limited shelf life (including garden plants/trees).] — [Rhododendronwal, Algemene voorwaarden](https://www.rhododendronwal.nl/algemene-voorwaarden)
- Rhodo-online, withdrawal allowed for pot/root-ball plants:
  - "Voor levende planten die gekweekt zijn in pot of als kluit geldt dat de planten in goede staat en onbeschadigd geretourneerd worden ... De planten dienen tussentijds met zorg uit de verpakking gehaald te worden en van water voorzien te worden" [Pot or root-ball plants must be returned in good, undamaged condition; meanwhile they must be carefully unpacked and watered]
  - "Planten die geleverd worden als wortelgoed/blote wortels geldt géén herroeppingsrecht." [No withdrawal right for bare-root plants.]
  - "Wij kunnen de planten weer afhalen na betaling van extra transportkosten of u zorgt zelf voor het retourneren." [We can collect the plants for an extra transport fee, or you return them yourself.]
  — [Rhodo-online, Refund policy](https://www.rhodo-online.nl/policies/refund-policy)
- Azaleashop: "kan de consument het herroepingsrecht NIET uitoefenen voor de producten die aangeboden worden door Azaleashop, omdat deze producten snel kunnen bederven en maar een beperkte houdbaarheid hebben." [The consumer can NOT exercise the withdrawal right because these products can spoil quickly.] On its own deliveries: "Bij levering door de verkoper is de schade opgelopen aan de goederen tijdens het transport de aansprakelijkheid van de verkoper. Elke zichtbare beschadiging ... moet door de klant onmiddellijk gemeld worden ... Bij afhaling door de koper is de verkoper niet verantwoordelijk voor de schade" [When the seller delivers, transport damage is the seller's liability; visible damage must be reported immediately; on collection by the buyer the seller is not responsible]. Complaints are not accepted for "beschadiging ... door opzet, grove onachtzaamheid of nalatig onderhoud" [damage from intent, gross negligence or neglected care]. — [Azaleashop, Algemene voorwaarden](https://azaleashop.be/nl/algemene-voorwaarden/)
- Plantenwelt Wiesmoor: the withdrawal page lists the statutory exclusion "Waren, die schnell verderben können" [goods that can spoil quickly], and the online withdrawal form states "alle Artikel vom Widerruf ausgeschlossen sind" [all items are excluded from withdrawal]. On transport damage: "Als Verbraucher werden Sie gebeten, die Ware bei Lieferung umgehend auf Vollständigkeit, offensichtliche Mängel und Transportschäden zu überprüfen und uns sowie dem Spediteur Beanstandungen schnellstmöglich mitzuteilen. Kommen Sie dem nicht nach, hat dies keine Auswirkung auf Ihre gesetzlichen Gewährleistungsansprüche." [Consumers are asked to check goods on delivery for completeness, obvious defects and transport damage and notify us and the carrier as soon as possible; failing to do so does not affect statutory rights.] On risk: "Soweit Sie Verbraucher sind ... [geht] die Gefahr ... erst mit der Übergabe der Ware an Sie" [for consumers, risk passes only on handover to you]. — [Plantenwelt Widerruf](https://plantenwelt.de/policies/refund-policy); [Plantenwelt AGB](https://plantenwelt.de/policies/terms-of-service)
- Böhlje: "Werden Waren mit offensichtlichen Transportschäden angeliefert, so reklamieren Sie solche Fehler bitte möglichst sofort beim Zusteller und nehmen Sie bitte unverzüglich Kontakt zu uns auf. Sie helfen uns aber, unsere eigenen Ansprüche gegenüber dem Frachtführer ... geltend machen zu können." [If goods arrive with obvious transport damage, please complain to the carrier immediately and contact us; failing to do so has no consequences for your statutory rights, but it helps us claim against the carrier.] — [pflanzen-boehlje.de AGB](https://www.pflanzen-boehlje.de/de-de/agb)
- Esveld: "Klachten over het vervoer dienen zowel bij de vervoerder (naam en adres staan altijd op de vrachtbrief) als bij ons, ook weer uitsluitend schriftelijk ingediend te worden." [Transport complaints must be filed in writing with both the carrier (name on the consignment note) and us.] Also: "Extreme weersomstandigheden kunnen sommige planten ernstig beschadigen, ook reeds verkochte ... nog niet geleverde artikelen. In dergelijke omstandigheden zijn wij van leveringsplicht ontslagen." [Extreme weather can damage plants already sold but not yet delivered; we are then released from the delivery obligation.] — [Esveld, Handelsvoorwaarden](https://esveld.nl/handelsvoorwaarden/)
- Hachmann, risk and insurance: risk passes to the customer on handover to the carrier only "wenn der Kunde den Spediteur ... mit der Ausführung beauftragt hat und wir dem Kunden diese Person ... nicht zuvor benannt haben" [if the customer commissioned the carrier and we did not name it]. "Eine Transportversicherung wird nur auf ausdrücklichen Wunsch und auf Kosten des Kunden abgeschlossen." [Transport insurance only on express request and at the customer's cost.] — [Hachmann AGB](https://www.hachmann.de/agb/)
- Hachmann, logistics for large rhododendrons: "Wenn das Auftragsvolumen mehr als 4 Kartons übersteigt oder in der Bestellung Rhododendron ab einer Größe von 50/60 cm enthalten sind, müssen wir eine Spedition beauftragen." [If an order exceeds 4 boxes or contains rhododendrons of 50/60 cm or more, we must use a freight forwarder.] Freight in Germany costs "derzeit bei 70,00 bis 90,00 €" [currently €70–90] and is free over €500. - Rhododendronwal, own transport and delivery:
  - "Omdat wij in 90% van alle gevallen de Rhododendrons met eigen vervoer bezorgen kunnen wij hier [leverdatum] vaak aan voldoen." [Because in 90% of cases we deliver with our own transport, we can often meet your chosen delivery date.]
  - "Hierover wordt altijd eerst contact opgenomen om de leverdatum af te stemmen." [We always contact you first to agree the delivery date.]
  - Delivery fee "€75,- voor Nederland en €99,- voor België" [€75 Netherlands, €99 Belgium]. The product-page FAQ says private sales deliver "uitsluitend binnen Nederland" [only within the Netherlands], another inconsistency.
  - After delivery: "De Rhododendrons worden bij ons altijd met kluit of pot bezorgd. Deze kunnen een tijd boven de grond blijven staan. let op met warm weer dat u deze zo nu en dan voldoende water geeft." [Always delivered with root ball or pot; they can stand above ground for a while; in warm weather water them regularly.]
  — [Rhododendronwal, Bezorgen en afhalen](https://www.rhododendronwal.nl/bezorgen-en-afhalen); [product page FAQ](https://www.rhododendronwal.nl/product/rhododendron-cosmopolitan-rood-roze)

### Inferences
- With its own transport, the Otterlo nursery is in the position Azaleashop describes: the seller carries transport-damage risk until handover. A simple "check at delivery together with our driver" step (sign-off or photo at handover) would replace the 48-hour photo rule that parcel shippers use. This is a suggestion based on the patterns above.
- On withdrawal: most specialists exclude live plants as perishable. Rhodo-online shows that a conditional return (pot/root-ball, unpacked and watered, customer pays return or pays for collection) is workable. Whichever is chosen, competitors' contradictions show the guarantee page and terms must say the same thing.

### Gaps
- Whether the perishable-goods exclusion legally covers hardy root-ball rhododendrons was not assessed here. Competitors take different positions; this needs legal review.
