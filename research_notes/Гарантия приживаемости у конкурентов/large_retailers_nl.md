# Plant guarantee, returns and problem-resolution policies of large NL garden retailers (benchmark for a rhododendron nursery in Otterlo)

Scope: consumer-facing garden-centre chains, DIY chains and big online plant shops selling to the Netherlands. Every page cited below was loaded on **16 Nov 2026** unless marked otherwise. Quotes are the original Dutch, followed by a short English gloss in brackets. This is internal benchmarking only, and the findings are reported neutrally.

Retailers covered: Intratuin, Hornbach NL, Bakker.com (NL site), Praxis, GroenRijk, Life & Garden, 123planten, Plantje.nl, Fluwel, plus Gamma/Karwei, Tuincentrum Osdorp and bol.com, where the pages could not be checked (noted as such). Also the shared garden-centre consumer terms (Tuinbranche Nederland / SER model).

## 1. Is there a growth or plant guarantee on garden plants? What is promised, and for how long?

### Takeaway
Only two of the large chains publish a real growth guarantee on outdoor plants. **Intratuin** gives 6 months and replaces the plant (in-store purchases only). **Hornbach** gives 2 years on hardy perennial plants planted in open ground, with replacement or refund at Hornbach's choice. The online plant specialists offer short guarantees of about **30 days**: Bakker (free 30 days, or 6 months for a €3.90 surcharge, settled as a credit voucher or replacement), 123planten (a discount on the next plant) and Plantje.nl. GroenRijk, Life & Garden and Praxis publish no plant guarantee beyond the statutory one. No retailer found uses the wording "1 jaar groeigarantie" today.

### Inferences
- The realistic baseline a customer compares against is: big garden centre = **6 months, new plant** (Intratuin); DIY chain = **2 years, replace or refund** (Hornbach, hardy plants in open ground only); online plant shops = **30 days**, often settled with **credit or a discount** rather than cash.
- A nursery guarantee of **1 year with a free replacement rhododendron** would sit above Intratuin and all online players, and below Hornbach's 2 years in headline length. Hornbach's exclusions (correct planting and care, no container plants) are broadly what a nursery would also need.
- Bakker's free guarantee appears to have shrunk from the multi-year promise remembered on forums to 30 days, plus a paid 6-month option. This suggests large online players treat long establishment guarantees as a cost to be priced, not a default. The old promise is unverified and of unknown date.
- Remedies vary: replacement (Intratuin, Bakker), replacement or refund at the seller's choice (Hornbach), voucher/credit (Bakker), discount (123planten). A plain "new plant or money back" offer would be among the more generous.

### Gaps
- Whether Hornbach's 2-year guarantee is still current. The PDF is dated V 01/2022, and hornbach.nl returned a bot-protection page on 16 Nov 2026, so the live site could not be checked. A search result for a Hornbach Buxus product page mentions a 2-year guarantee, but I could not open it.
- Gamma and Karwei: every page returned HTTP 429 (blocked) on 16 Nov 2026. I found no evidence of a plant growth guarantee in search results.
- GroenRijk is a franchise formula. Individual stores may run their own growth guarantees, which I could not check.
- Plantje.nl's "30 dagen plantgarantie" has no published terms that I could find (remedy, conditions).
- What "LDF" means in Bakker's exclusion is not explained on the page. Products shipped directly by growers are excluded, so LDF may be a related category.
- Tuincentrum Osdorp: on 16 Nov 2026 tuincentrumosdorp.nl redirected to a page with unrelated content, not the garden centre's site, so I found no policy. The Radar quote above says garden-centre guarantees differ per branch.
- bol.com plant sellers: I found no verified bol.com or seller policy on plant guarantees. Guarantees are set per marketplace seller.
- I found no Consumentenbond, Radar or Kassa comparison of plant guarantees across retailers.

## 2. Conditions, exclusions and how to claim (receipt, photos, bringing the plant back, deadlines)

### Takeaway
Garden-centre chains require **the physical plant plus proof of purchase** at the store: Intratuin asks for plant, label and receipt; Hornbach for the receipt, invoice or e-receipt plus the item. Online sellers work by **e-mail with photos and the order number**. Exclusions centre on incorrect planting or care, plants grown in pots/containers (Hornbach), force majeure and deliberate damage. Some online sellers add their own conditions, such as no repotting within 30 days (123planten) or "no proof needed" only under the paid tier (Bakker).

### Inferences
- In-store claim norms are "bring the plant + label + receipt back to the store of purchase". A nursery could match this, or be more lenient, for example by accepting photos and the order number by e-mail or WhatsApp instead of a trip to Otterlo.
- "Niet correct geplant en verzorgd" (Hornbach) and "ondeskundig gebruik of gebrek aan zorgvuldigheid" (branch terms) are the standard carve-outs. A nursery guarantee with explicit rhododendron care conditions (acid soil, watering in the first summer) would be consistent with market practice.
- Container/potted plants are excluded at Hornbach. That is relevant for rhododendrons sold for patio pots.

### Gaps
- No chain publishes a deadline for reporting a failed plant inside the guarantee window, beyond "tijdig" [in good time]. None specifies whether the failed plant must be dug up and brought back for large shrubs. Intratuin requires "de plant"; Hornbach requires "vertoon van het artikel" [showing the item].
- Gamma, Karwei and the live hornbach.nl pages could not be loaded (see section 1 gaps).

## 3. Returns of live plants and the right of withdrawal online; damaged-on-arrival procedures

### Takeaway
Most large sellers **exclude live plants from the 14-day right of withdrawal** as perishable goods: Bakker, Life & Garden, Praxis (despite advertising 30/90 days generally), 123planten for fresh plants, and Plantje.nl for flowering plants. **Intratuin's webshop** is the exception and accepts plant returns within **7 days**, although its own service page contradicts this. In store, the branch terms used by GroenRijk and Life & Garden exclude plants from the 10-day exchange right, while Intratuin allows plant exchanges within 7 days. Damaged-on-arrival handling is photo-based everywhere. The only explicit deadline found is **48 hours** (Plantje.nl).

### Cited Findings
**Intratuin (online)**
- Webshop FAQ: "Ben je niet tevreden over de ontvangen plant? Dan kun je deze in de meeste gevallen retourneren, maar dit moet wel binnen 7 dagen. … Je kunt de plant(en) ook terugbrengen naar een willekeurige Intratuin-vestiging." [Not happy with the plant? In most cases you can return it, within 7 days, also to any Intratuin store.] — [Intratuin – FAQ retourneren webshop](https://www.intratuin.nl/klantenservice/faq-retourneren-webshop)
- The same FAQ lists general non-returnables: "Producten die snel kunnen bederven of een beperkte houdbaarheid hebben." [Products that spoil quickly or have limited shelf life.] Refund of shipping: "Eventuele verzendkosten worden alleen terugbetaald wanneer de volledige bestelling wordt geretourneerd." [Shipping refunded only if the whole order is returned.] — [same page](https://www.intratuin.nl/klantenservice/faq-retourneren-webshop)
- Contradicting service page: "Planten en levend groen die besteld en geleverd zijn via de webshop kunnen niet worden geretourneerd of geruild." ## 4. How prominently the guarantee is advertised (homepage USP bar, product page badge, checkout)

### Takeaway
Only the **online houseplant specialists** put a guarantee in the header USP bar: 123planten "30 dagen groen garantie", Plantje.nl "30 dagen plantgarantie". ## 5. Differences between in-store and online purchases

### Takeaway
The clearest split is at **Intratuin**: the 6-month growth guarantee and plant exchanges apply to **store purchases**, and the service page explicitly excludes **webshop plants** from the guarantee. **Hornbach's** guarantee covers "alle bij HORNBACH gekochte" qualifying plants and accepts an online invoice or e-receipt, which suggests it covers both channels. Most chains give *longer* return windows online than the statutory 14 days (Praxis 30/90, Hornbach 30), but carve live plants out of them. Branch-level terms (GroenRijk, Life & Garden) leave policy to each store.

### Cited Findings
- Intratuin: "Let op: planten die besteld en geleverd zijn via de webshop kunnen niet worden geretourneerd of geruild en hierop geldt onze groengarantie dus niet." [Plants ordered and delivered via the webshop cannot be returned or exchanged, so our green guarantee does not apply to them.] — [Intratuin – Garantie en service](https://www.intratuin.nl/informatie/garantie-en-service)
- Intratuin in store: "Planten die zijn gekocht in een Intratuin-vestiging kunnen binnen 7 dagen na aankoop worden geruild of geretourneerd. Hiervoor heb je de originele bijbehorende kassabon nodig." [Plants bought in an Intratuin store can be exchanged or returned within 7 days with the original receipt.] The webshop FAQ separately allows 7-day plant returns by post or in any store (see section 3). — [Intratuin – Retourneren winkel aankopen](https://www.intratuin.nl/klantenservice/faq-retourneren-winkel); [Intratuin – FAQ retourneren webshop](https://www.intratuin.nl/klantenservice/faq-retourneren-webshop)
- Intratuin webshop refunds made in store: "Let op: In de winkel ontvang je geen directe terugbetaling." [In the store you get no immediate refund.] Online orders ship from individual stores: "Retourzendingen moeten altijd worden teruggestuurd naar de vestiging van waaruit de plant is verzonden." [Returns must go to the branch that shipped the plant.] — [Intratuin – FAQ retourneren webshop](https://www.intratuin.nl/klantenservice/faq-retourneren-webshop)
- Hornbach: the guarantee covers "alle bij HORNBACH gekochte meerjarige, winterharde planten in de volle grond" and proof can be "de originele kassabon of de originele factuur resp. de e-bon uit het klantaccount" [the original receipt, invoice, or e-receipt from the customer account]. — [Hornbach plantengarantie (PDF)](https://media.hornbach.nl/cms/nl/plantengarantie-012022-nl.pdf)
- Praxis: returns are accepted in any store for online purchases: "Retourneren mag in elke gewenste Praxis winkel. Ook als je het artikel niet in dat filiaal of online* gekocht hebt." [Return to any Praxis store, also if bought at another branch or online.] The Praxis Plus terms state: "Je kunt aankopen binnen 90 dagen retourneren (in plaats van 30 dagen voor winkelaankopen, en 14 dagen voor online aankopen)." [90 days instead of 30 for store purchases and 14 for online purchases.] This conflicts with the 30-day online window stated on the returns page. Living products are excluded in both cases. — [Praxis – Retourneren](https://www.praxis.nl/service/retourneren); [Praxis – Algemene voorwaarden](https://www.praxis.nl/algemene-voorwaarden)
- Life & Garden online returns go "naar de vestiging waar u het heeft gekocht" [to the branch you bought it from], again showing branch-level handling. — [Life & Garden – Bezorgen en retourneren](https://www.lifeandgarden.com/bezorgen-en-retourneren)
- Bakker is online-only. Its guarantees are tied to "na ontvangst van je bestelling" / "na levering" [after receipt or delivery of the order], not the purchase date. — [Bakker – Garantie & kwaliteit](https://nl-nl.bakker.com/pages/garantie-en-kwaliteit)

### Inferences
- Shoppers who buy rhododendrons online from chains often have **less** protection than in-store buyers: Intratuin's growth guarantee does not apply, and plants are usually excluded from the withdrawal right. A nursery that sells by mail and offers the same guarantee online as at the farm gate would compare well.
- Chains whose online orders are fulfilled by individual stores or by suppliers (Intratuin, Life & Garden) route returns and complaints to that store or supplier. A family nursery offering one direct contact person is a practical, comparable service point.
- Starting the guarantee at delivery (Bakker) rather than at purchase (Intratuin, Hornbach) matters for mail-order plants and is a detail worth deciding on.

- GroenRijk's webshop route (groenrijk.nl/webshop) redirected to a 404 page on 16 Nov 2026. I could not establish whether GroenRijk currently sells plants online centrally.
- I found no information on bol.com marketplace sellers' in-store equivalents, as most are online-only nurseries.
