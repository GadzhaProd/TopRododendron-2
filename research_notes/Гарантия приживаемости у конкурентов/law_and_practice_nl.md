# Selling live garden plants online with a growth guarantee (aangroeigarantie): Dutch law and best practice (as of November 2026)

Scope: a small family rhododendron nursery in Otterlo (NL). It sells online to consumers and businesses, delivers with its own transport across the Netherlands, and offers pick-up. This is information, not legal advice. Points marked "LAWYER CHECK" should be confirmed by a Dutch consumer-law lawyer before the terms go live.

Method note: the Civil Code (BW) texts below were pulled verbatim from wetten.overheid.nl on 16-11-2026. Boek 6 was shown as "Geldend van 16-07-2026 t/m heden", with "Wijziging(en) zonder datum inwerkingtreding aanwezig". Boek 7 was the version at 2026-07-01. EUR-Lex could not be reached from this environment, so the EU directive articles are cited through their Dutch implementation, not their own text.

Correction to the brief: the 14-day withdrawal rules are in **Boek 6** BW (afdeling 6.5.2B, art. 6:230g–6:230z), not Boek 7. The conformity and guarantee rules are in Boek 7 (art. 7:5 ff.). Art. 7:46a–46j ("Overeenkomsten op afstand") is marked "[Vervallen per 13-06-2014]" in the current Boek 7. Those rules moved to Boek 6 when the Consumer Rights Directive was implemented, so sources citing 7:46a ff. are outdated. — [BW Boek 7, afdeling 9A](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)

## 1. Is there a 14-day right of withdrawal (herroepingsrecht) for live plants bought online, or does the perishable-goods exception apply?

### Takeaway
By default the 14-day withdrawal right applies to online sales to consumers. The statutory exception covers goods "die snel bederven of die een beperkte houdbaarheid hebben" ("that spoil quickly or have a limited shelf life"). The ACM's example list names flowers, not plants, and no Dutch authority or court ruling was found that clearly puts hardy container-grown shrubs such as rhododendrons inside that exception. Relying on it is therefore a legal risk rather than a certainty. Since 19 June 2026 webshops must also offer an online withdrawal button (herroepingsknop).

### Cited Findings
- **Default rule (14 days from receipt).** Art. 6:230o lid 1 BW: "De consument kan een overeenkomst op afstand ... zonder opgave van redenen ontbinden tot een termijn van veertien dagen is verstreken, na: ... b. bij een consumentenkoop: 1°. de dag waarop de consument of een door de consument aangewezen derde, die niet de vervoerder is, de zaak heeft ontvangen". Gloss: a consumer may cancel a distance contract without giving reasons within 14 days of receiving the goods. — [BW Boek 6, art. 6:230o (wetten.overheid.nl)](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/)
- **Statutory exception (exact wording).** Art. 6:230p BW: "De consument heeft geen recht van ontbinding bij: ... f. een consumentenkoop betreffende: ... 2°. de levering van zaken die snel bederven of die een beperkte houdbaarheid hebben". Gloss: no withdrawal right for goods that spoil quickly or have a limited shelf life. Related exceptions in the same article: 1° made to the consumer's specifications; 4° goods "die na levering door hun aard onherroepelijk vermengd zijn met andere zaken" (goods that by their nature are irreversibly mixed with other items after delivery). — [BW Boek 6, art. 6:230p](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/)
- **ACM's paraphrase and examples.** The ACM's trader page says there is in any case no cooling-off period for "producten die snel bederven of verouderen, zoals vers voedsel, verse dranken of bloemen" (products that spoil or age quickly, such as fresh food, fresh drinks or flowers). The same list gives "producten die bij levering mengen met iets anders: bijvoorbeeld beton of zand storten in een tuin" (pouring concrete or sand into a garden). Plants are not named. — [ACM, Bedenktijd (verkoop aan consumenten)](https://www.acm.nl/nl/verkoop-aan-consumenten/klantenservice/bedenktijd)
- **Thuiswinkel.org standard terms (2025 template)**, art. 10: the trader may exclude "Producten die snel bederven of een beperkte houdbaarheid hebben", "maar alleen als de ondernemer dit duidelijk bij het aanbod, althans tijdig voor het sluiten van de overeenkomst, heeft vermeld" (but only if this was clearly stated with the offer, or at least in good time before the contract was concluded). — [Algemene Voorwaarden Thuiswinkel 2025 (as reproduced by a member webshop)](https://static.werkaandemuur.nl/cms/algemene-voorwaarden-2025.pdf)
- **You must tell the consumer if there is no withdrawal right.** Art. 6:230m lid 1 sub k BW requires, before the contract, "indien er niet voorzien is in het recht van ontbinding van de overeenkomst, de informatie dat de consument geen recht van ontbinding heeft". Gloss: where no withdrawal right exists, the trader must say so. — [BW Boek 6, art. 6:230m](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/)
- **Penalty for missing withdrawal information.** Art. 6:230o lid 2 BW: if the withdrawal information required by 6:230m lid 1 sub h is missing, the 14-day term is extended until it is supplied, "doch met ten hoogste twaalf maanden" (by at most twelve months). The ACM also warns that the cooling-off period can be "automatisch verlengd tot één jaar" and that fines are possible. — [BW Boek 6, art. 6:230o](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/); [ACM news 11-06-2026](https://www.acm.nl/nl/publicaties/acm-roept-online-retailers-op-zich-voor-te-bereiden-op-herroepingsknop)
- **Commentary that plants fall under the exception (old source).** An ICTRecht blog (14 Dec 2012, by a former employee) said the exception for goods "die snel kunnen bederven of verouderen" also covers "planten en sommige cosmeticaproducten". It predates the 2014 implementation of the CRD, has no case law behind it, and notes that the boundary is unclear. — [ICTRecht, Uitsluiting herroepingsrecht bederfelijke waren (2012)](https://www.ictrecht.nl/blog/uitsluiting-herroepingsrecht-bederfelijke-waren-hoe-zit-het-ook-al-weer)
- **Belgian lawyer's view (analogous EU-harmonised rule).** Dirk Martens (advocaat, Gent), writing in Sierteelt & Groenvoorziening (1 April 2016), says the exception covers "voedingswaren, bloemen en sommige planten" (food, flowers and *some* plants) and that it is "zeer beperkt toegepast" (applied very narrowly). Non-perishable items such as plant pots remain returnable within 14 days, and the trader must still inform the consumer in advance, for example by correct labelling. This is Belgian law, but it implements the same Directive 2011/83/EU. — [Martens, "Wettelijke garantie en herroepingsrecht voor de verkoop van planten" (WUR e-depot)](https://edepot.wur.nl/382435)
- **Sector practice (webshops, not authorities).** Many Dutch plant webshops exclude live plants from withdrawal. Directplant cites the limited shelf life of garden plants but keeps seeds, bulbs, soil products and fertiliser (if unopened) returnable. Plantsico states that the exclusion does not remove legal rights for damaged or wrong deliveries. These are traders' own positions. — [Directplant algemene voorwaarden](https://www.directplant.nl/klantenservice/algemene-voorwaarden); [Plantsico retourbeleid](https://plantsico.nl/pages/retourbeleid)
- **When it is a "distance contract" (relevant to pick-up).** Art. 6:230g lid 1 sub e BW defines an overeenkomst op afstand as one concluded "zonder gelijktijdige persoonlijke aanwezigheid van handelaar en consument en waarbij, tot en met het moment van het sluiten van de overeenkomst, uitsluitend gebruik wordt gemaakt van een of meer middelen voor communicatie op afstand". Gloss: a distance contract is concluded without the trader and consumer being physically together, using only distance communication up to the moment of conclusion. — [BW Boek 6, art. 6:230g](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/)
- **New in 2026: the withdrawal button (herroepingsknop).** Art. 6:230oa lid 1 BW: "De handelaar zorgt ervoor dat de consument het recht van ontbinding van een via een online-interface gesloten overeenkomst op afstand kan uitoefenen door een verklaring tot ontbinding in te dienen door middel van een duidelijk zichtbaar op de online-interface weergegeven en gemakkelijk toegankelijke functie." Gloss: online traders must offer a clearly visible, easily accessible cancellation function. Lid 4 requires immediate confirmation on a durable medium. Lid 5 requires the function to be available "gedurende deze termijn te allen tijde" (at all times during the withdrawal period). — [BW Boek 6, art. 6:230oa](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/)
- **ACM on the button.** It is mandatory from 19 June 2026: "Vanaf 19 juni 2026 moeten webshops en apps een duidelijke zogenoemde 'ontbindings- of herroepingsknop' hebben." Suggested label: "mijn aankoop ongedaan maken". "Inloggen mag als optie worden aangeboden, maar het aanmaken van een account mag niet verplicht worden gesteld." (Logging in may be offered as an option, but creating an account may not be required.) It applies only to online purchases. — [ACM news, 11-06-2026](https://www.acm.nl/nl/publicaties/acm-roept-online-retailers-op-zich-voor-te-bereiden-op-herroepingsknop)
- **Date conflict.** A search snippet of the government's Ondernemersplein page reportedly gives 25 June 2026 as the effective date, while the ACM and most other sources say 19 June 2026 (from Directive (EU) 2023/2673). I did not verify the Ondernemersplein page myself. — [Ondernemersplein: webshops moeten een herroepingsknop hebben](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/webshops-moeten-een-herroepingsknop-hebben/)

### Inferences
- A hardy rhododendron grown in a container can usually stay healthy in its pot for weeks if watered. A strict reading of "snel bederven / beperkte houdbaarheid" therefore fits it less well than cut flowers, fresh produce, or freshly dug bare-root and root-ball (kluit) stock in the planting season. Because exceptions are read narrowly (Martens) and the ACM names only flowers, a blanket "no withdrawal on all plants" clause carries real risk. If a dispute body finds the exception does not apply, the consumer gets withdrawal rights extended by up to 12 months (6:230o lid 2), and the ACM could act. LAWYER CHECK.
- A defensible middle path that also builds trust:
  - (a) Apply the exception only to clearly perishable lines (bare-root, freshly dug root-ball plants, cut flowers or greens), and label this per product, as Thuiswinkel art. 10 requires.
  - (b) Offer container-grown plants with a 14-day withdrawal right, or a voluntary 14-day return, as long as the plant is unplanted, in its original pot and well cared for. The law already lets the trader deduct for value loss from handling beyond what is needed to judge the goods (6:230s; not extracted here, LAWYER CHECK).
  - (c) Always allow withdrawal on non-living items (pots, soil, fertiliser, tools).
- **Pick-up.** If the consumer orders and pays online and only collects at the nursery, the contract was concluded entirely at a distance, so it remains a distance contract and the 14 days start on collection. If the customer picks and buys in person at the nursery, it is not a distance contract and there is no withdrawal right. Click-and-collect orders therefore also need the withdrawal button.
- **The button.** The webshop needs it in any case if it sells any item that carries a withdrawal right, such as pots, soil or gift vouchers. Whether it must also be shown for orders consisting only of excluded items is a technical point. LAWYER CHECK.
- **Delivery with the nursery's own transport.** Under 7:11 BW (see Q2), risk passes only when the consumer receives the goods, so transport damage is always the nursery's risk.

### Gaps
- No Dutch court ruling or Geschillencommissie decision was found that specifically decides whether potted garden shrubs are "snel bederfelijk".
- No current ConsuWijzer page specifically about plants was retrieved. The ACM trader page was used instead.
- The Thuiswinkel.org text was read from a member's reproduction of the 2025 template, not from thuiswinkel.org itself.
- The exact Directive 2011/83 art. 16(d) wording ("goods which are liable to deteriorate or expire rapidly") could not be fetched from EUR-Lex in this session.

## 2. The legal conformity guarantee (wettelijke garantie / conformiteit): what it means for plants that die after delivery, burden of proof, and the 1-year presumption

### Takeaway
Every consumer sale carries a mandatory conformity guarantee. The plant must have the qualities the buyer may reasonably expect, and for a hardy shrub that plausibly includes being healthy and able to establish with normal care. If a defect shows up within **one year** of delivery, it is presumed to have existed at delivery unless the seller proves otherwise, or unless "de aard van de zaak of de aard van de afwijking zich daartegen verzet" (the nature of the goods or of the defect is incompatible with the presumption). This presumption has applied since 1 January 2022; it was 6 months before. In practice, the law already gives consumers much of what a "1-year aangroeigarantie" promises, so a weak commercial guarantee adds little.

### Cited Findings
- **Conformity requirement.** Art. 7:17 lid 1 BW: "De afgeleverde zaak moet aan de overeenkomst beantwoorden." (The delivered goods must conform to the contract.) Lid 2: "Een zaak beantwoordt niet aan de overeenkomst indien zij, mede gelet op de aard van de zaak en de mededelingen die de verkoper over de zaak heeft gedaan, niet de eigenschappen bezit die de koper op grond van de overeenkomst mocht verwachten. De koper mag verwachten dat de zaak de eigenschappen bezit die voor een normaal gebruik daarvan nodig zijn ...". Gloss: the goods do not conform if, given their nature and the seller's statements, they lack the properties the buyer was entitled to expect, including those needed for normal use. — [BW Boek 7, art. 7:17 (wetten.overheid.nl)](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **One-year presumption (current text).** Art. 7:18 lid 2 BW: "Bij een consumentenkoop wordt vermoed dat de zaak ... bij aflevering niet aan de overeenkomst beantwoordt, indien de afwijking van hetgeen is overeengekomen zich binnen één jaar na aflevering openbaart, tenzij de verkoper anders aantoont of de aard van de zaak of de aard van de afwijking zich daartegen verzet." Gloss: in a consumer sale, a defect that appears within one year of delivery is presumed to have existed at delivery, unless the seller proves otherwise or the nature of the goods or of the defect is incompatible with this. — [BW Boek 7, art. 7:18](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **The change from 6 to 12 months.** Since 2022 the presumption period is 12 months; older sources still say 6 months. — [Rechtshulp Rotterdam, Conformiteit en garantie](https://rechtshulprotterdam.nl/encyclopedie/conformiteit-garantie-consument). The 2021 Geschillencommissie ruling below still applied the six-month rule, which is consistent with the change taking effect for contracts from 1 January 2022 (Directive (EU) 2019/771). — [De Geschillencommissie, 26-01-2021](https://www.degeschillencommissie.nl/uitspraken/ondeugdelijk-product-ondernemer-moet-bewijzen-dat-product-bij-aflevering-geen-gebreken-vertoonde/)
- **Mandatory law.** Art. 7:6 lid 1 BW: "Bij een consumentenkoop kan van de afdelingen 1-7 van deze titel niet ten nadele van de koper worden afgeweken en kunnen de rechten en vorderingen die de wet aan de koper ter zake van een tekortkoming ... toekent, niet worden beperkt of uitgesloten." Gloss: in consumer sales, these rules cannot be departed from to the buyer's detriment, and the buyer's statutory remedies cannot be limited or excluded. — [BW Boek 7, art. 7:6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **Remedies.** Art. 7:21 lid 1 BW: the buyer may demand "b. herstel" (repair) or "c. vervanging van de afgeleverde zaak" (replacement). Lid 2: "De kosten van nakoming ... kunnen niet aan de koper in rekening worden gebracht" (the costs of performance cannot be charged to the buyer). Lid 3: performance must take place "binnen een redelijke termijn en zonder ernstige overlast voor de koper" (within a reasonable time and without serious inconvenience to the buyer). Under lid 1 sub c, replacement is not owed if the item deteriorated because the buyer did not look after it carefully once he should have reckoned with returning it. — [BW Boek 7, art. 7:21](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **Notice period.** Art. 7:23 lid 1 BW: "Bij een consumentenkoop moet de kennisgeving binnen bekwame tijd na de ontdekking geschieden, waarbij een kennisgeving binnen een termijn van twee maanden na de ontdekking tijdig is." Gloss: a consumer must notify within a reasonable time after discovering the defect, and notice within two months of discovery is always timely. Lid 2: claims become time-barred two years after that notice. — [BW Boek 7, art. 7:23](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **Transfer of risk on home delivery.** Art. 7:11 lid 1 BW: "Bij een consumentenkoop waarbij de zaak bij de koper wordt bezorgd, is de zaak voor het risico van de koper vanaf het moment dat de koper ... de zaak heeft ontvangen." Gloss: when goods are delivered, risk passes only on receipt by the consumer. — [BW Boek 7, art. 7:11](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **No fixed statutory guarantee period in NL.** Art. 7:17 contains no time limit. The consumer guide KLACHT.nl notes that the legal guarantee "kent geen vaste termijn" (has no fixed term); it depends on the expected lifespan. — [KLACHT.nl, Defect product na garantieperiode](https://www.klacht.nl/consumentenrecht/defect-product-na-garantieperiode-rechten/)
- **Key dispute precedent on a tree that did not establish (Geschillencommissie Webshop, 26 Jan 2021, ref. 34031/37873, bindend advies, upheld).**
  - Facts: two leilindes (€239.90) were bought in Feb 2019; one never leafed out. The consumer reported this within 6 months. The trader told them to wait through summer and then the next spring, and later blamed the dry summer, too little watering, and planting between paving on sand. It also argued that its one-year growth guarantee had expired.
  - Reasoning: the Commission applied the (then 6-month) presumption. The seller must at least make it plausible that the product was sound at delivery, and had not done so. The location and drought arguments were raised only afterwards, and the trader "had never actually inspected the consumer's situation".
  - On the trader's own purchase guarantee: a trader who wants to deny a claim must show explicitly that a specific condition was not met, based on facts rather than assumptions or photos.
  - Outcome: the trader had to deliver a replacement tree of the same specification within four weeks and refund €27.50 complaint fee. — [De Geschillencommissie, uitspraak 26-01-2021](https://www.degeschillencommissie.nl/uitspraken/ondeugdelijk-product-ondernemer-moet-bewijzen-dat-product-bij-aflevering-geen-gebreken-vertoonde/)
- **Belgian view on "nature of the goods" for perishables.** Under the Belgian equivalent, a rebuttal of the presumption is more readily accepted for goods that spoil quickly or have limited shelf life, "wat ook voor sommige planten het geval is" (which is also the case for some plants). The author advises enclosing clear planting and storage instructions on a durable carrier, attached to the plants, so that buyer errors can be shown. He also notes that the legal guarantee cannot be shortened in the terms. — [Martens 2016, Sierteelt & Groenvoorziening (WUR e-depot)](https://edepot.wur.nl/382435)
- **A growers' guarantee does not replace the law.** One Dutch trader states that the legal guarantee still applies to all items, and that a 100% guarantee on houseplants is not feasible because care cannot be controlled. — [Plantada, Garantie & retourneren](https://plantada.nl/garantie-retourneren/)

### Inferences
- **For a rhododendron that dies within 12 months of delivery**, the starting point is that it is presumed non-conforming. The nursery can escape this only by proving the plant was sound, or by showing that the cause lies in the nature of the defect, for example clear frost damage, drought stress, waterlogging, planting in calcareous soil, or plain neglect. As the 2021 ruling shows, this must rest on facts gathered at the time, ideally an inspection or detailed photos. Raising "it was the weather" months later does not work.
- **After 12 months**, the consumer must prove the plant was defective at delivery. That is hard for a living plant, so a commercial guarantee that runs beyond 12 months, or that drops the need to prove cause, creates the most real added value.
- **Rhododendron-specific facts to state on the product page and in care sheets.** What the buyer "may expect" is shaped by the seller's statements (7:17 lid 2), so these facts both limit disputes and set expectations: lime-free, acidic soil (pH roughly 4.5–5.5); no planting in heavy clay without soil improvement; watering during the first season; shade or half-shade needs per cultivar; hardiness rating.
- **Remedy.** For a dead plant the practical remedy is replacement (7:21 sub c) free of charge (7:21 lid 2), including delivery cost. Refund or price reduction follows if replacement fails or is unreasonable.
- **The nursery's own transport** means any root-ball damage or heat stress in transit is clearly its risk (7:11). Delivery checklists signed by the customer (condition on handover) are useful evidence.

### Gaps
- No court ruling (as opposed to Geschillencommissie advice) on plant conformity in NL was found.
- How Dutch courts treat the "aard van de zaak" exception for living plants under the new 12-month rule is untested in the sources found. LAWYER CHECK.

## 3. Legal guarantee vs commercial guarantee (handelaars-/fabrieksgarantie): what a commercial guarantee must state

### Takeaway
A commercial guarantee (commerciële garantie) is a voluntary extra promise. It binds the guarantor on the terms in the guarantee **and in related advertising**, and it must never limit or replace the legal guarantee. Art. 7:6a BW sets out its required content. It must be given on a durable medium at the latest on delivery. From 27 September 2026, sellers must also show the EU **harmonised notice** about the legal guarantee. Presenting legal rights as if they were a special perk is always a misleading practice.

### Cited Findings
- **Binding force, and binding by advertising.** Art. 7:6a lid 1 BW: the buyer may exercise the rights in a commercial guarantee "onverminderd alle andere rechten of vorderingen die de wet de koper toekent. De commerciële garantie is bindend onder de voorwaarden in het commerciële garantiebewijs en in de daarmee samenhangende reclame die beschikbaar was bij of voor het sluiten van de overeenkomst." Gloss: guarantee rights come on top of all statutory rights, and the guarantee binds on the terms in the guarantee statement and in related advertising available at or before the contract. — [BW Boek 7, art. 7:6a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **Durable medium.** Art. 7:6a lid 3: "Het commerciële garantiebewijs wordt de koper verstrekt op een duurzame gegevensdrager, uiterlijk bij de levering van de zaak." Gloss: the guarantee statement must be given on a durable medium (e.g. PDF or e-mail) at the latest on delivery. — [BW art. 7:6a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **Mandatory content.** Art. 7:6a lid 4: "In de commerciële garantie staat op duidelijke en begrijpelijke wijze welke ... rechten of vorderingen de koper zijn toegekend en dat deze de koper toekomen onverminderd de rechten of vorderingen die de wet hem toekent. Voorts bevat het garantiebewijs:
  - a. een duidelijke verklaring van de rechten of vorderingen die de koper kosteloos toekomen als de zaak niet aan de overeenkomst beantwoordt en dat deze niet worden aangetast door de commerciële garantie;
  - b. de naam en het adres van de verkoper of de producent;
  - c. de procedure die de koper moet volgen om de nakoming van de commerciële garantie te verkrijgen;
  - d. de aanduiding van de onder de commerciële garantie vallende zaak of zaken;
  - e. de commerciële garantievoorwaarden."

  Gloss: in clear and understandable language: what rights the guarantee gives and that they are in addition to statutory rights; plus (a) a clear statement of the free legal remedies for non-conformity and that the guarantee does not affect them, (b) the guarantor's name and address, (c) the claim procedure, (d) which goods are covered, and (e) the guarantee terms. — [BW art. 7:6a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **Non-compliance does not release the guarantor.** Art. 7:6a lid 5: "Niet-naleving door de verkoper van de vereisten bedoeld in het vorige lid heeft geen invloed op de bindende aard van de commerciële garantie." — [BW art. 7:6a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **Advertising claims count.** Art. 7:6a lid 6: guarantee rights also apply if the goods lack properties promised "in een reclame door de verkoper of producent", unless the advertising was corrected before the contract in a similar way. — [BW art. 7:6a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **Definition.** Art. 7:6a lid 7 sub a: "commerciële garantie: iedere verbintenis van de verkoper of een producent om aan de koper de betaalde prijs terug te betalen of de zaak te vervangen, te herstellen of te onderhouden, wanneer die niet voldoet aan de specificaties of andere vereisten, die vermeld zijn in het garantiebewijs ...". Gloss: any undertaking to refund, replace, repair or maintain when the goods do not meet the specifications in the guarantee statement. — [BW art. 7:6a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **Producer's durability guarantee.** Art. 7:6a lid 2: if "de producent" offers a commercial guarantee of durability for a period, the producer is directly liable for repair or replacement during that period. — [BW art. 7:6a](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **Pre-contract information.** Art. 6:230m lid 1 sub m BW: the trader must inform about "het bestaan en de voorwaarden van ... commerciële garanties". — [BW Boek 6, art. 6:230m](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/)
- **New 2026 harmonised notice about the legal guarantee.** Art. 6:230m lid 1 sub l BW now requires "een herinnering aan het bestaan van de wettelijke regeling om een zaak te leveren die beantwoordt aan de overeenkomst en de belangrijkste elementen daarvan, die op een in het oog springende manier wordt verstrekt met behulp van de kennisgeving, bedoeld in artikel 22 bis van de richtlijn". Gloss: a reminder of the legal conformity guarantee and its main elements, shown prominently using the EU harmonised notice. — [BW Boek 6, art. 6:230m](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/)
- **Origin, timing and format of the notice.** It comes from Directive (EU) 2024/825 (EmpCo). It applies from 27 September 2026, online and offline. Its design and text are fixed in Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1960. A separate harmonised label (the "GARAN" label) applies to producers' commercial guarantees of durability. — [Fieldfisher: New EU guarantee notice and GARAN label](https://www.fieldfisher.com/en/insights/new-eu-guarantee-notice-and-garan-label-is-your-business-ready); [Noerr: new labelling obligations under the EmpCo Directive](https://www.noerr.com/en/insights/new-labelling-obligations-for-commercial-guarantees-offered-by-producers-and-legal-guarantee-of-conformity-under-the-empco-directive-action-needed-by-september-2026); [Cuatrecasas: Harmonized notice and label 2026](https://www.cuatrecasas.com/en/latam/retail-1/art/harmonized-notice-label-new-eu-rules-2026)
- **Blacklisted practice.** Art. 6:193g BW: "De volgende handelspraktijken zijn onder alle omstandigheden misleidend: ... j. wettelijke rechten van consumenten voorstellen als een onderscheidend kenmerk van het aanbod van de handelaar". Gloss: always misleading to present consumers' statutory rights as a distinctive feature of the trader's offer. — [BW Boek 6, art. 6:193g](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/)
- **Thuiswinkel standard clause.** Art. 12 lid 2: "Een door de ondernemer, diens toeleverancier, fabrikant of importeur verstrekte extra garantie beperkt nimmer de wettelijke rechten en vorderingen die de consument op grond van de overeenkomst tegenover de ondernemer kan doen gelden ...". Lid 3 defines "extra garantie" as rights "die verder gaan dan waartoe deze wettelijk verplicht is" (that go beyond what the trader is legally obliged to provide). — [Algemene Voorwaarden Thuiswinkel 2025](https://static.werkaandemuur.nl/cms/algemene-voorwaarden-2025.pdf)
- **Right to repair (marginal for plants).** Directive (EU) 2024/1799 had to be transposed by 31 July 2026. The Netherlands missed that deadline; the bill (Implementatiewet richtlijn bevordering van reparatie, Kamerstukken 36 996) was still before parliament in autumn 2026. — [Dutch IT Channel, 3 Aug 2026: Nederland mist deadline](https://www.dutchitchannel.nl/news/756926/nederland-mist-deadline-voor-europees-recht-op-reparatie); [Consumentenbond: recht op reparatie komt later](https://www.consumentenbond.nl/acties-claims/nieuws/2026/recht-op-reparatie-komt-later)

### Inferences
- **What the law already gives (legal guarantee):**
  - a plant that conforms (healthy, true to name and size, fit to establish with normal care);
  - free replacement if it does not;
  - the seller bears the burden of proof for 12 months.
- **What an aangroeigarantie can add (commercial guarantee):**
  - (i) **no proof of cause**: replacement even if the customer cannot show the plant was faulty, possibly including mild care mistakes;
  - (ii) **a longer period**: e.g. until 1 July or 1 September of the year after planting, or 2 years;
  - (iii) **an easier procedure**: photo plus order number via a web form, a decision within X days;
  - (iv) **service extras**: free replacement delivery by the nursery's own van in the planting season, aftercare advice, or a choice between replacement and store credit or refund.
- **Wording that just restates the legal guarantee is risky.** A "1-year guarantee: we replace the plant if it was defective on delivery" adds nothing, and marketing it as a special feature risks a breach of 6:193g sub j. Heavy conditions inside the first year can even make the "guarantee" look worse than the law, which erodes trust and invites ACM attention.
- **Marketing must not over-promise.** Because advertising binds (7:6a lid 1 and lid 6), slogans like "100% aangroeigarantie" or "altijd gratis vervanging" must match the actual conditions. Otherwise the more generous advertised version may be enforced. LAWYER CHECK: Directive 2019/771 art. 17(1) says that if the guarantee statement is less favourable than the advertising, the advertising prevails. I could not fetch that text in this session.
- **Territorial scope.** 7:6a lid 4 does not list it explicitly; the old Directive 1999/44 did. It is still good practice to state it, since the nursery delivers only in NL. A suitable sentence: "geldt voor planten die door ons in Nederland zijn afgeleverd of in Otterlo zijn opgehaald" (applies to plants delivered by us in the Netherlands or collected in Otterlo).
- **The nursery is both grower ("producent") and seller.** If it offers a free durability guarantee covering the whole plant for a long period, check whether the EU harmonised label obligation under Directive 2024/825 / Reg. 2025/1960 applies. My understanding is that it applies to producer durability guarantees longer than two years, but I did not verify that threshold in the primary text. LAWYER CHECK.
- The harmonised notice about the legal guarantee must now appear on the webshop, at least prominently before checkout, and at the pick-up point in Otterlo.

### Gaps
- The exact Dutch text of the harmonised notice (Reg. 2025/1960) and its required placement in a webshop were not retrieved.
- The precise scope and threshold of the GARAN label obligation, and whether a grower's aangroeigarantie counts as a "commercial guarantee of durability", were not verified.
- The text of Directive 2019/771 art. 17 could not be fetched from EUR-Lex here.

## 4. Rules for algemene voorwaarden (general terms) on guarantees and exclusions: black and grey lists

### Takeaway
Towards consumers, terms cannot limit or exclude the legal conformity guarantee (7:6 BW). Clauses that shift the burden of proof, leave it to the seller to decide whether it failed to perform, or shorten limitation periods to under a year are on the **black list** (6:236 BW). Clauses that materially limit the seller's obligations, exclude statutory damages, or forfeit rights as a sanction are **presumed unfair** (grey list, 6:237 BW). A voluntary extra guarantee may have conditions, but they must be clear (6:238 lid 2) and must not leak into the legal guarantee.

### Cited Findings
- **Black list (6:236 BW), always unreasonably onerous towards a consumer.** Opening words: "Bij een overeenkomst tussen een gebruiker en een wederpartij, natuurlijk persoon, die niet handelt in de uitoefening van een beroep of bedrijf, wordt als onredelijk bezwarend aangemerkt een in de algemene voorwaarden voorkomend beding ..." Relevant items:
  - **b.** "dat de aan de wederpartij toekomende bevoegdheid tot ontbinding ... uitsluit of beperkt" (excludes or limits the consumer's right to rescind);
  - **d.** "dat de beoordeling van de vraag of de gebruiker in de nakoming van een of meer van zijn verbintenissen is te kort geschoten aan hem zelf overlaat ..." (leaves it to the trader itself to judge whether it has failed to perform);
  - **g.** "dat een wettelijke verjarings- of vervaltermijn ... tot een verjarings- onderscheidenlijk vervaltermijn van minder dan een jaar verkort" (shortens a statutory limitation period to less than a year);
  - **k.** "dat de bevoegdheid van de wederpartij om bewijs te leveren uitsluit of beperkt, of dat de uit de wet voortvloeiende verdeling van de bewijslast ten nadele van de wederpartij wijzigt ..." (limits the consumer's right to prove, or shifts the statutory burden of proof against the consumer).
  — [BW Boek 6, art. 6:236](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/)
- **Grey list (6:237 BW), presumed unreasonably onerous:**
  - **b.** "dat de inhoud van de verplichtingen van de gebruiker wezenlijk beperkt ten opzichte van hetgeen de wederpartij ... zonder dat beding redelijkerwijs mocht verwachten" (materially limits the trader's obligations compared with what the consumer could reasonably expect);
  - **f.** "dat de gebruiker of een derde geheel of ten dele bevrijdt van een wettelijke verplichting tot schadevergoeding" (releases the trader from statutory liability for damages);
  - **h.** "dat als sanctie op bepaalde gedragingen van de wederpartij, nalaten daaronder begrepen, verval stelt van haar toekomende rechten ..., behoudens voor zover deze gedragingen het verval van die rechten ... rechtvaardigen" (forfeits the consumer's rights as a sanction for certain conduct, including omissions, unless that conduct justifies the forfeiture).
  — [BW Boek 6, art. 6:237](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/)
- **Clarity and interpretation.** Art. 6:238 lid 2 BW: "Bij een overeenkomst als bedoeld in de artikelen 236 en 237 moeten de bedingen duidelijk en begrijpelijk zijn opgesteld. Bij twijfel over de betekenis van een beding, prevaleert de voor de wederpartij gunstigste uitleg." Gloss: consumer terms must be clear and understandable, and in case of doubt the interpretation most favourable to the consumer prevails. — [BW Boek 6, art. 6:238](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/)
- **Providing the terms.** Art. 6:234 BW: the terms must be provided before or at conclusion of the contract, electronically in the manner of 6:230c for online contracts. Otherwise they can be voided. — [BW Boek 6, art. 6:234](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/)
- **Mandatory consumer sales law and notice periods.** See 7:6 lid 1 (no deviation to the buyer's detriment) and 7:23 lid 1 (notice within two months of discovery is always timely), quoted in Q2. — [BW Boek 7](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **The legal guarantee cannot be shortened by terms** (Belgian commentary on the same EU rules): e.g. "slechts 8 maanden" instead of the legal term is not allowed. — [Martens 2016](https://edepot.wur.nl/382435)

### Inferences
Examples of clauses and how they likely fare towards consumers. LAWYER CHECK on each before use.

**Not allowed or very risky:**
- "Op levende planten wordt geen garantie gegeven." (No guarantee on live plants.) Void against consumers under 7:6.
- "Klachten over dode of zieke planten moeten binnen 48 uur na levering met foto's worden gemeld, anders vervalt elk recht." (Complaints must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photos, otherwise all rights lapse.) As applied to the legal guarantee, this conflicts with 7:23 (two months after discovery) and 7:6, and also touches grey item h. It is acceptable only as a condition of the *extra* guarantee, and only if the text makes clear that legal rights remain.
- "Wij beoordelen of de plant juist is verzorgd; ons oordeel is bindend." (We judge whether the plant was cared for correctly; our judgement is binding.) Black list 6:236 d.
- "De klant moet aantonen dat de plant volgens onze instructies is verzorgd." (The customer must show the plant was cared for according to our instructions.) Within the first 12 months this reverses the 7:18 lid 2 presumption and hits black list 6:236 k as applied to the legal guarantee. It can only be a condition of the voluntary extra guarantee.
- "Aansprakelijkheid is beperkt tot de factuurwaarde." (Liability is limited to the invoice value.) Grey 6:237 f towards consumers.

**Generally acceptable:**
- Reasonable *extra-guarantee* conditions, clearly separated from the legal guarantee: planting within X days of receipt, following the enclosed care sheet, suitable lime-free soil, watering in dry periods, and reporting within a reasonable period with photos and order number. In a voluntary benefit these are not black-listed, but they must be clear (6:238 lid 2), and the guarantee text must state that legal rights are unaffected (7:6a lid 4).
- **Weather exclusions** in the extra guarantee, such as extreme frost below a stated hardiness rating, prolonged drought without watering, flooding, storm, or damage by animals or vandalism. Phrase them specifically and objectively, not as blanket "overmacht" (force majeure). Under the legal guarantee, the nursery must still prove such a cause within the first 12 months.

### Gaps
- No Dutch case law found applying 6:236 or 6:237 specifically to plant-guarantee clauses.
- The ACM has not published plant-sector guidance on unfair terms (none found).

## 5. B2B (hoveniers, companies): what is usual

### Takeaway
Business buyers have **no withdrawal right**: the distance-selling rules protect only "consumenten". The mandatory consumer-sale rules (7:6, 7:18 lid 2 presumption, 7:23 two-month rule) also do not apply, so contractual freedom is wide. Sector terms for B2B nursery stock (Anthos, Plantum) typically exclude any guarantee of growth or flowering and impose short complaint periods. One important constraint is statutory: a hovenier who has to compensate its own consumer customer can claim recourse from its supplier, and that right **cannot be excluded** (7:25 BW).

### Cited Findings
- **Who is protected.** Art. 6:230g lid 1 sub a BW: "consument: iedere natuurlijke persoon die handelt voor doeleinden die buiten zijn bedrijfs- of beroepsactiviteit vallen". Art. 7:5 lid 1 sub a BW: a consumentenkoop is a sale by a professional seller to "een koper, natuurlijk persoon, die handelt voor doeleinden buiten zijn bedrijfs- of beroepsactiviteit". Gloss: only natural persons buying outside their trade are consumers, so withdrawal and mandatory consumer-sale rules do not cover hoveniers or companies. — [BW Boek 6, art. 6:230g](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/); [BW Boek 7, art. 7:5](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **B2B notice rule.** Art. 7:23 lid 1 BW: the buyer loses the right to invoke non-conformity "indien hij de verkoper daarvan niet binnen bekwame tijd nadat hij dit heeft ontdekt of redelijkerwijs had behoren te ontdekken, kennis heeft gegeven". Gloss: in B2B, notice must be given within a reasonable time after the defect was or should have been discovered, which implies a duty to inspect. — [BW Boek 7, art. 7:23](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **Anthos terms (B2B, members only).**
  - Art. 1.1: "Deze voorwaarden zijn alleen toepasselijk op overeenkomsten waarbij een van de partijen ... lid is van Anthos" (these terms apply only where one party is an Anthos member).
  - Art. 7.3: complaints about quality and quantity "moeten schriftelijk en uiterlijk binnen acht kalenderdagen na aflevering zijn ingediend" (in writing, within eight calendar days of delivery). Hidden defects must be reported "terstond na ontdekking ... doch in ieder geval voor het einde van het eerste groeiseizoen na aflevering" (immediately on discovery, and in any case before the end of the first growing season after delivery).
  - Art. 7.4: the seller must be given the chance to investigate; expert inspection costs fall on the losing side.
  - Art. 7.5: remedy at the seller's choice of supplying what is missing, replacement, or a proportional refund.
  - Art. 8.1: "De verkoper zal nimmer aansprakelijk zijn voor de hergroei of bloei van de geleverde goederen. Het is te allen tijde de verantwoordelijkheid van de koper te beoordelen of de omstandigheden, waaronder de klimatologische, geschikt zijn voor de goederen." Gloss: the seller is never liable for regrowth or flowering, and it is always the buyer's responsibility to judge whether conditions, including climate, suit the goods.
  - Art. 8.2: "De verkoper staat in voor de soortechtheid van de door hem geleverde planten." (The seller guarantees the plants are true to species.)
  — [Anthos Algemene verkoop- en leveringsvoorwaarden (bundled PDF via Green-Works)](https://green-works.nl/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Algemene-verkoop-en-leveringsvoorwaarden-l-Anthos-Plantum.pdf)
- **Plantum terms (starting material and plants; deposited 21 May 2012; same PDF).**
  - Art. 10.3: "De verkoper staat niet in voor groei en bloei van de geleverde producten." (The seller does not guarantee growth and flowering.)
  - Art. 10.6–10.7: visible defects within two days, in writing within eight days; hidden defects within two days of discovery.
  - Art. 11.1: liability capped at invoice value, and no consequential loss.
  — [Plantum voorwaarden in the same PDF](https://green-works.nl/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Algemene-verkoop-en-leveringsvoorwaarden-l-Anthos-Plantum.pdf)
- **Recourse right of a reselling hovenier (cannot be excluded).** Art. 7:25 lid 1 BW: if a consumer buyer has exercised rights against the seller, "dan heeft de verkoper recht op schadevergoeding jegens degene van wie hij de zaak heeft gekocht, mits ook deze ... in de uitoefening van zijn beroep of bedrijf heeft gehandeld". Lid 2: "Van lid 1 kan niet ten nadele van de verkoper worden afgeweken." Lid 3 excludes recourse where the cause arose after delivery to the intermediate seller. — [BW Boek 7, art. 7:25](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- **Small business buyers and unfair-terms protection.** Art. 6:235 lid 1 BW: the general unfair-terms grounds (6:233, 6:234) cannot be invoked by companies that publish annual accounts or have 50 or more employees. Small hoveniers can still invoke them. — [BW Boek 6, art. 6:235](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/)

### Inferences
- **Use separate B2B terms** that:
  - state clearly that no withdrawal right applies;
  - set a short inspection and complaint period for visible defects (e.g. 2–8 days, in line with Anthos and Plantum);
  - set a period for hidden defects up to the end of the first growing season;
  - guarantee trueness to name and quality grade, but not growth or flowering;
  - cap liability at invoice value.
  This mirrors sector norms.
- **Commercially, a hovenier may value a limited B2B aangroeigarantie,** for example a replacement-only guarantee for plants planted by the hovenier per a planting protocol, claimed within the first growing season. It lets them pass a guarantee on to their consumer clients. A clause trying to exclude 7:25 recourse would be void (lid 2).
- **When a business buys "as a consumer"** (e.g. an owner buying for a private garden), consumer rules apply. The checkout should ask for company name, KvK number and VAT number to classify B2B orders.
- **Whether 7:25 applies** when the hovenier's contract with the consumer is a works contract (aanneming van werk: supply and plant) rather than a sale is a legal question. LAWYER CHECK.

### Gaps
- Invoice and VAT requirements for B2B sales were not researched; they are tax rules, outside this scope.
- No source was found on what share of Dutch nurseries offer B2B growth guarantees.
- The "reflexwerking" (indirect effect) of the black and grey lists for small businesses is a known doctrine, but no source was retrieved in this session.

## 6. Best practice in the green and webshop sector: codes, claim procedures, fair vs customer-hostile conditions

### Takeaway
The relevant codes are the Thuiswinkel.org standard terms and the Thuiswinkel Waarborg (a dispute body, a 14-day complaint response, and Thuiswinkel guaranteeing binding advice up to €10,000), the VHG consumer terms for hoveniers (agreed with the Consumentenbond), and the Anthos and Plantum terms for B2B. The Geschillencommissie's 2021 tree ruling is the clearest guide to what is fair in practice: claims must be investigated promptly and on facts, and vague after-the-fact weather or care excuses fail. No sector-specific "aangroeigarantie code" from Tuinbranche Nederland, Groenkeur or Anthos was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Thuiswinkel.org standard terms (2025).**
  - Art. 16 lid 2: complaints must be filed "binnen bekwame tijd nadat de consument de gebreken heeft geconstateerd, volledig en duidelijk omschreven" (within a reasonable time after discovery, fully and clearly described).
  - Art. 16 lid 3: complaints are answered "binnen een termijn van 14 dagen gerekend vanaf de datum van ontvangst" (within 14 days of receipt), or a holding reply is sent with an indication of timing.
  - Disputes go to the Geschillencommissie.
  - Thuiswinkel.org "staat garant voor de nakoming van de bindende adviezen" (guarantees compliance with binding advice) up to €10,000 per binding advice.
  — [Algemene Voorwaarden Thuiswinkel 2025](https://static.werkaandemuur.nl/cms/algemene-voorwaarden-2025.pdf)
- **VHG consumer terms (hoveniers).** These were agreed with the Consumentenbond and took effect on 1 January 2019. They include a nakomingsgarantieregeling (performance guarantee scheme), with disputes decided by a geschillencommissie. — [VHG Algemene Consumentenvoorwaarden (Law Insider copy)](https://lawinsider.com/nl/contracts/1obTPOJK4Gm)
- **Fair claim handling per the Geschillencommissie (2021, tree that did not establish).** The trader lost because it:
  - (a) told the customer to wait for a season and then another instead of assessing the claim;
  - (b) raised drought and location only afterwards;
  - (c) never inspected;
  - (d) relied on assumptions and photos rather than showing a specific guarantee condition was not met.
  — [De Geschillencommissie, 26-01-2021](https://www.degeschillencommissie.nl/uitspraken/ondeugdelijk-product-ondernemer-moet-bewijzen-dat-product-bij-aflevering-geen-gebreken-vertoonde/)
- **Planting and care instructions as risk management.** Attach clear, durable, plant-specific planting and storage instructions (drawings are acceptable) to the plants, and refer to them in the terms. — [Martens 2016](https://edepot.wur.nl/382435)
- **B2B sector norms.** Short complaint windows, no guarantee of growth or flowering, and trueness to species guaranteed. — [Anthos and Plantum terms](https://green-works.nl/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Algemene-verkoop-en-leveringsvoorwaarden-l-Anthos-Plantum.pdf)
- **Practitioner framework for effective guarantees.** Hart (HBR, July 1988): a good guarantee is unconditional, easy to understand, meaningful, easy to invoke and easy to collect on. — [HBR, "The Power of Unconditional Service Guarantees" (1988)](https://store.hbr.org/product/power-of-unconditional-service-guarantees/88405)
- **Retailer practice.** Some plant webshops say a 100% guarantee is not possible because care cannot be controlled. — [Plantada](https://plantada.nl/garantie-retourneren/)

### Inferences

**Fair conditions** (widely seen as reasonable and consistent with the sources):
- a photo of the whole plant, the label and the planting spot, plus order number;
- reporting within a reasonable period after the problem is noticed. Generous options: "binnen 4 weken na constatering" (within 4 weeks of noticing), or before a fixed seasonal cut-off;
- planting within roughly 1–2 weeks of receipt, or keeping the plant watered in its pot;
- suitable acidic, lime-free soil, which the nursery should explain and can sell;
- watering during the first growing season;
- objective exclusions: frost beyond the stated hardiness, flooding, animal or mechanical damage, transplanting by the customer;
- replacement delivered free with the next own-transport run in planting season, with a refund or credit option if the cultivar is unavailable;
- a decision within 14 days; inspection on site if the nursery doubts the claim (feasible with own transport);
- a written statement that legal rights are unaffected.

**Customer-hostile conditions** (likely to backfire or be unlawful):
- 24–48 hour photo deadlines for any claim;
- requiring receipts for soil or fertiliser purchases;
- requiring return of the dead plant at the customer's cost;
- "only if planted by a professional hovenier" for consumer sales;
- blanket "weersomstandigheden" (weather conditions) or "overmacht" exclusions;
- the nursery's sole discretion over claims (black list 6:236 d);
- replacement only "at our choice" with a cheaper size;
- shipping charged on replacements;
- guarantee voided by any deviation from the care sheet;
- telling the customer to "wait and see" for a season (rejected by the Geschillencommissie).
- **Thuiswinkel Waarborg membership** (or at least joining the Geschillencommissie Webshop) and showing the logo is a recognised Dutch trust signal. The €10,000 binding-advice guarantee is a concrete benefit to cite.

### Gaps
- No published code of conduct or guarantee standard from Tuinbranche Nederland, Groenkeur/TuinKeur, Anthos, or KAVB was found specifically for consumer aangroeigaranties.
- I did not systematically survey competitor guarantee texts; the parallel competitor research covers this.
- No ConsuWijzer or ACM guidance specific to plant guarantees was found.

## 7. Consumer psychology and conversion: do guarantees and risk-reversal raise conversion and trust, and do vague or heavily conditioned guarantees backfire?

### Takeaway
Moderate-to-strong evidence (a meta-analysis plus experiments) shows that more lenient money-back and return terms raise purchase intent and purchases more than they raise returns, and that money-back guarantees work partly through positive emotion. Return or guarantee policy is a measurable checkout-abandonment factor: in Baymard's survey, 13% of cart abandoners cited an unsatisfactory returns policy. Evidence that conditions "backfire" is more mixed. Unconditional guarantees are generally preferred, but clear, specific conditions can be seen as fair and more credible, especially for experience goods and to limit opportunism. What hurts is **vagueness and hassle**, not specificity as such.

### Cited Findings
- **[Strong: meta-analysis] Janakiraman, Syrdal & Freling (2016), Journal of Retailing 92(2): 226–235.** A meta-analysis of 21 papers. Overall, return-policy leniency increases purchases more than it increases returns. Money and effort leniency raise purchases; scope leniency increases returns; time and exchange leniency reduce returns. — [EconPapers record](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jouret:v:92:y:2016:i:2:p:226-235); [ScienceDaily summary (Jan 2016)](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2016/01/160119141921.htm)
- **[Moderate: two experiments] Suwelack, Hogreve & Hoyer (2011), Journal of Retailing 87(4): 462–478.** Money-back guarantees create a positive emotional response that raises purchase intention and willingness to pay a premium. They work for both search and experience goods, but experience goods need stricter return conditions, and return conditions matter in guarantee design. — [IDEAS/RePEc record](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jouret/v87y2011i4p462-478.html)
- **[Moderate: industry survey, large sample, self-report] Baymard Institute.** Average documented cart abandonment is 70.22% (50 studies). Among abandoners, 13% cited "Returns policy wasn't satisfactory" and 19% "I didn't trust the site with my credit card information". The page does not give sample size or year for the reasons breakdown. — [Baymard, Cart abandonment rate statistics](https://baymard.com/blog/cart-abandonment-rate-statistics)
- **[Moderate, not verified on the page itself] Baymard product-page benchmark.** Per search summaries, about 60% of shoppers look for return information on the product page, while 44% of sites do not show or link it there. I could not confirm this on the Baymard page. — [Baymard, footer and return links article](https://baymard.com/blog/footer-needs-return-shipping-links)
- **[Moderate: classic experiment] Boulding & Kirmani (1993), Journal of Consumer Research 20(1): 111–123.** Consumers perceive warranties as quality signals, and the effect depends on the firm's credibility. This characterisation comes from citing papers; I did not read the original abstract. — [EconPapers record](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:ucp:jconrs:v:20:y:1993:i:1:p:111-23)
- **[Moderate: experiment] McDougall, Levesque & VanderPlaat (1998), Journal of Services Marketing.** Respondents preferred firms with unconditional guarantees, but specific guarantees were preferred when respondents considered how easy it was to actually get money back. — [Emerald, doi 10.1108/08876049810226955](https://www.emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/08876049810226955/full/html)
- **[Weak to moderate: qualitative focus groups] Björlin Lidén & Edvardsson (2003), Managing Service Quality.** Customers preferred detailed rules on when a guarantee applies. Where a firm has a poor reputation, an unconditional guarantee can seem like a "rip-off", i.e. not credible. — [Emerald, doi 10.1108/09604520310495822](https://www.emeraldinsight.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/09604520310495822/full/html)
- **[Moderate: cross-national survey, 171 respondents, 23 countries] Van Vaerenbergh, De Keyser & Larivière (2014), Managing Service Quality.** Unconditional guarantees can invite opportunistic claims among some customer groups, so conditional guarantees may suit firms worried about abuse. — [Emerald, doi 10.1108/MSQ-06-2013-0115](https://www.emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/MSQ-06-2013-0115/full/html)
- **[Weak to moderate: factorial experiment, services] Study in the Journal of Consumer Satisfaction, Dissatisfaction and Complaining Behavior.** After a complaint and recovery, a conditional guarantee improved perceived quality for some services, while an unconditional one had no effect. Guarantees used on their own have limited benefits. — [JCSDCB, "The Effects of Service Guarantees on Service Evaluations During a Voiced Complaint and Service Recovery"](https://jcsdcb.com/index.php/JCSDCB/article/view/54)
- **[Practitioner framework, not empirical] Hart (1988, HBR).** Effective guarantees are unconditional, easy to understand, meaningful, easy to invoke, and easy to collect. Secondary claims attributing "+10–15% retention" to unconditional guarantees are unsourced and should not be used. — [HBR store listing](https://store.hbr.org/product/power-of-unconditional-service-guarantees/88405)

### Inferences
- **For an experience good like a garden plant**, where success shows only months later and depends partly on the customer, the evidence favours a guarantee that is:
  - generous on money and effort: free replacement, free re-delivery, a simple photo claim;
  - specific and transparent about a few objective conditions: planting time, acidic soil, watering;
  - long enough to cover the first full growing season.

  This matches Suwelack et al. (experience goods need conditions, but conditions should not erode the emotional reassurance) and Janakiraman et al. (money and effort leniency drive purchases).
- **Backfire risk.** The evidence points to vagueness ("onder voorwaarden" without listing them), hidden fine print, hassle, and conditions that make the guarantee worse than the legal right. That combination fails Hart's "easy to invoke and collect" test, and in NL it also creates legal exposure (6:193g j, 6:238 lid 2).
- **Credibility matters** (Boulding & Kirmani; Björlin Lidén & Edvardsson). A small family nursery can strengthen credibility with:
  - real faces and the Otterlo location;
  - reviews mentioning guarantee claims handled well;
  - Thuiswinkel Waarborg or Geschillencommissie membership;
  - concrete numbers, e.g. "in 2025 we replaced X of Y plants". Use only true figures.
- **Placement.** Baymard's data suggests the guarantee and return terms should be summarised on the product page and in the cart, with a link to the full text, not only in the footer or terms.

### Gaps
- No Nielsen Norman Group study specifically on guarantees or risk-reversal was found in this session.
- No study specific to plants or horticulture e-commerce on guarantees and conversion was found.
- No A/B-test data from Dutch webshops was found; vendor claims seen in search results were unverified and excluded.
- Effect sizes from Janakiraman et al. and Suwelack et al. were not extracted, since the full texts are paywalled.

## 8. How to word a fair, legally sound and trust-building aangroeigarantie (synthesis)

### Takeaway
Build the guarantee as a clearly *extra* promise on top of the legal guarantee. Meet every item in art. 7:6a lid 4 BW. Make it generous where the law is weakest (after 12 months, and no proof of cause needed) and simple to claim. Keep conditions few, objective and rhododendron-specific. Never present legal rights as the perk. Have a Dutch consumer-law lawyer review the final text.

### Cited Findings
- Required elements: rights given, "onverminderd" (without prejudice to) statutory rights; a statement on free legal remedies; name and address; procedure; covered goods; and terms. It must be on a durable medium by delivery at the latest. — [BW art. 7:6a leden 3–4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- The guarantee and related advertising bind. — [BW art. 7:6a leden 1 and 6](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005290/2026-07-01)
- Clarity, and the consumer-friendly interpretation in case of doubt. — [BW art. 6:238 lid 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/)
- Never present legal rights as a distinctive feature. — [BW art. 6:193g sub j](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/)
- Pre-contract information on guarantees, plus the harmonised legal-guarantee notice (from 27-09-2026). — [BW art. 6:230m lid 1 sub l and m](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289/); [Fieldfisher](https://www.fieldfisher.com/en/insights/new-eu-guarantee-notice-and-garan-label-is-your-business-ready)
- Fair claim handling: assess promptly and on facts, inspect if in doubt, and do not rely on hindsight excuses. — [Geschillencommissie 26-01-2021](https://www.degeschillencommissie.nl/uitspraken/ondeugdelijk-product-ondernemer-moet-bewijzen-dat-product-bij-aflevering-geen-gebreken-vertoonde/)
- Effective guarantee traits: unconditional or simple, meaningful, easy to invoke and collect. — [Hart 1988, HBR](https://store.hbr.org/product/power-of-unconditional-service-guarantees/88405)

### Inferences
**Illustrative skeleton**, Dutch with English gloss. This is a draft for a lawyer to review, not final legal text.

1. **Kop en belofte** (heading and promise): "Aangroeigarantie TopRododendron: slaat uw rododendron niet aan, dan krijgt u gratis een nieuwe." Gloss: if your rhododendron does not establish, you get a new one free.
2. **Relation to the law (required by 7:6a lid 4 and sub a):** "Deze aangroeigarantie geldt bovenop uw wettelijke rechten. Ook zonder deze garantie heeft u recht op een plant die beantwoordt aan de overeenkomst; is dat niet zo, dan heeft u kosteloos recht op vervanging, of als dat niet mogelijk is op prijsvermindering of ontbinding. Deze garantie beperkt die rechten niet." Gloss: the guarantee is on top of statutory rights; even without it you are entitled to a conforming plant and free remedies; this guarantee does not limit those rights.
3. **Guarantor (sub b):** "[Bedrijfsnaam], [adres], Otterlo, KvK [nr], [e-mail/telefoon]." Gloss: company name, address in Otterlo, Chamber of Commerce number, contact details.
4. **Covered goods (sub d):** "Alle rododendrons en azalea's die u bij ons koopt als particulier, door ons in Nederland bezorgd of in Otterlo opgehaald." Gloss: all rhododendrons and azaleas bought from us as a private customer, delivered by us in NL or collected in Otterlo. Exclusions: cut greenery, and plants sold as "tweede keus/opruiming" (second grade or clearance) if they are clearly labelled as such.
5. **Duration:** "tot en met 31 augustus van het jaar na aankoop" (until 31 August of the year after purchase). This covers one full growing season after planting, whether planted in autumn or spring. The alternative is a flat 2 years. Either exceeds the 12-month presumption, so it adds real value.
6. **What we do:**
   - a free replacement plant of the same cultivar and size, or the closest available, delivered free with our own transport in the next planting season, or ready for pick-up;
   - if replacement is impossible: refund or credit, at the customer's choice.
7. **A few objective conditions (sub e).** Planting according to the enclosed care sheet means:
   - in lime-free, acidic soil;
   - within 14 days of receipt, or kept watered in the pot;
   - water during dry spells in the first year.

   Not covered:
   - frost below the stated hardiness rating;
   - flooding;
   - damage by animals, machines or vandalism;
   - plants moved after planting.
8. **How to claim (sub c):** "Meld het via [formulier/e-mail] zodra u merkt dat de plant niet goed gaat, met 2–3 foto's (hele plant, blad/knoppen, plantplek) en uw ordernummer. Wij reageren binnen 5 werkdagen en beslissen uiterlijk binnen 14 dagen. Twijfelen wij, dan komen we kijken; dat kost u niets." Gloss: report as soon as you notice a problem, with 2–3 photos and your order number; we reply within 5 working days and decide within 14 days; if in doubt we visit at no cost. This matches Thuiswinkel's 14-day complaint norm.
9. **Disputes:** reference to the Geschillencommissie (if a member) and to the harmonised legal-guarantee notice.
10. **Delivery of the text:** send the guarantee as a PDF with the order confirmation (a durable medium, 7:6a lid 3), print it on the care card, and summarise it on product pages and in the cart.

**Optional trust boosters** that fit the evidence: a "no-quibble first season" (replace without checking conditions in the first 6 months); publishing the claim rate; and a separate "plant-coach" contact.

**B2B variant:** replacement-only, for plants planted by the professional per the protocol, claim before the end of the first growing season, and no consequential loss. Recourse under 7:25 BW cannot be excluded.

  - (d) the exact harmonised-notice implementation.
- Commercial cost modelling (expected claim rate for rhododendrons, cost per replacement) was outside this research and should be estimated from the nursery's own failure data.
