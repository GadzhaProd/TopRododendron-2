## 1. Do they offer a growth guarantee? What is it called and what exactly is promised?

### Takeaway
Most of the dedicated hedge and shrub webshops advertise a guarantee. They use different names: "aangroeigarantie", "groeigarantie", "hergroeigarantie", "100% groeigarantie" or "gezondheidsgarantie". The remedy is usually **free replacement plants**. Some shops offer **replacement or refund**, a few use **sliding-scale refunds**, and a minority give **no guarantee on living plants**. One gives a discount on a new plant instead. Nobody offers store credit as the main remedy.

### Inferences
- The **dominant market pattern is "3 months, free replacement, correct planting required"**: Haag-planten.nl, Tuinplantenwinkel, Aalst, Rhododendronwal, Haagplantdirect, Portugese laurier kwekerij and Haagplantenwinkel all follow it. The longer, **season-based** models (until 1 June / end of first growing season) come from the larger tree and hedge sellers: Bomenbezorgd, Directplant, Haagplantenkopen and Bomenenzo.
- Shops that use "100%" in the name (Bomenbezorgd, Bomenenzo, Gardline month 1) still attach long exclusion lists. Several no-guarantee or partial sellers explicitly argue that "100% garantie" is impossible (Haagplantenkopen, Esveld, Plantcentraal, Haagplanten.net). So "100%" is a marketing claim that the sector itself disputes.
- At least three shops show **internal inconsistencies** between USP and terms (Tuinplant.nl 3 vs 2 months; Aalst 3 months vs one month; Rhododendronwal 30 days vs 3 months). This is a concrete pitfall to avoid. One consistent term stated identically everywhere would stand out.
- The closest direct competitor for a rhododendron nursery in Otterlo is Rhododendronwal in Wekerom, a rhododendron specialist that ships via PostNL. Both villages lie in the municipality of Ede (background knowledge). Its offer is 3 months, replacement or money back, and planting per its instructions.

Terms range from 1 month (Haagaanplanten in summer, Gardline at full value) to 5 months (Gras & Haag). Three shops tie the end to a fixed calendar date ("tot 1 juni", "vóór 15 juni"). Bomenbezorgd uses delivery-month bands. Most terms count **from delivery or purchase**. Haagplantenwinkel counts **from the order date**, and only Haagaanplanten counts **from planting**, because it does the planting itself.

### Inferences
- A flat "3 months" works badly for **autumn and winter deliveries of field-grown plants**: you can't judge a dormant plant until spring. The more carefully drafted policies solve this with a calendar end-date (Directplant, Haagplantenkopen: 1 June; Bomenenzo: 15 June; Bomenbezorgd: delivery-month table) or an explicit winter-hardy extension (Tuinplantenwinkel). Rhododendrons are evergreen and sold with root ball or in pots, and failures often show in the first dry spring or summer, so this is directly relevant.
- Several shops deliberately **exclude or shorten summer** (Haagplantenwinkel: no guarantee for kluit plants delivered 1 Jun–10 Sep; Haagaanplanten: only 1 month for Mar–Aug planting) because they see drought after planting as the dominant cause of failure.

### Gaps
- Most shops say "na aankoop" or "na levering" loosely. None defines precisely when a claim counts as "on time" (date of first e-mail vs date of photos).

## 3. Conditions and exclusions

### Takeaway
The near-universal conditions are: planted and cared for according to the shop's instructions; correct watering (neither too little nor too much); suitable soil and drainage. Weather extremes (frost, drought, storm, hail), animals, pests and diseases are excluded. Shop-specific conditions include a planting deadline after delivery (1 day at Haag-planten.nl, 5 days at Bomenbezorgd), root-ball handling rules, tree stakes, no planters or gravel beds, buying a named soil product, and planting done by the shop. Bare-root stock is often excluded.

A rhododendron nursery should state clearly what to do with the root ball and wrapping, so the condition cannot be disputed later.
- **Self-pickup vs delivered** is never singled out as an exclusion on the pages read. The pickup option shows up instead in **how replacements are handed over** (see Q4).

### Gaps
- No shop specifies ericaceous or acid-soil requirements (relevant to rhododendrons) as a guarantee condition. Rhododendronwal refers to its own planting instructions page, which was not read in detail.
- No page set a size limit for the guarantee (e.g., only up to X cm). Bare root vs kluit vs pot is the only stock-type differentiator found.

## 4. How to claim

### Takeaway
A claim usually means e-mailing photos plus the order number, or filling in a web form. Some shops add a reporting deadline (Haagplantenwinkel: within 2 months of delivery) or calendar cut-offs (Bomenenzo: 15 June / 15 July). The most rigorous claim process is Haag-planten.nl's form, which asks 14 questions about planting and watering and requires a photo of the underside of the root ball. Replacement shipping is free at Tuinplantenwinkel. At Haag-planten.nl and Haagplantdirect the replacement is collected free, or delivered at the customer's cost. Bomenbezorgd does not take dead plants back.

- Haagaanplanten: "Bij een melding vragen wij om foto's en andere informatie over de planten en de omstandigheden voor een goede beoordeling van de situatie." — [Haagaanplanten garantie](https://www.haagaanplanten.nl/garantie-en-herroeping)
- Haagplanten.net (search snippet, unverified): "Om in aanmerking te komen voor garantie ontvangen wij uw melding met ordernummer en drie foto's van de haag." [order number and three photos of the hedge]. — [bol.com Haagplanten.net (snippet)](https://www.bol.com/nl/v/Haagplanten-net/754214/)
- Esveld: "Klachten over de kwaliteit van de geleverde planten dienen zo spoedig mogelijk, doch uiterlijk binnen 30 dagen na levering, uitsluitend schriftelijk te worden ingediend … Er worden geen klachten in behandeling genomen als er geen aankoopbewijs overlegd kan worden!" Plants that are not true to type: claims allowed "tot een maximum van 7 jaar na de levering" and the plants should "zo mogelijk te worden geretourneerd". — [Esveld handelsvoorwaarden](https://esveld.nl/handelsvoorwaarden/)

[Only with original receipt and on showing the item; the replaced item becomes Hornbach's property.] — [Hornbach PDF](https://media.hornbach.nl/cms/nl/plantengarantie-012022-nl.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Three claim-intake models**: (a) a structured form with diagnostic questions (Haag-planten.nl; Bomenbezorgd and Aalst use forms with photo upload); (b) e-mail with order number and 1–3 photos (Directplant, Haagplantdirect, Haagplantenwinkel, Bomenenzo, Haagplanten.net); (c) written complaint within N days plus proof of purchase (Esveld, Hornbach). Model (a) also teaches the customer what "correct care" means and gathers the evidence needed to judge exclusions.
- Who pays for the replacement delivery is a real differentiator: free delivery (Tuinplantenwinkel), free collection or paid delivery (Haag-planten.nl, Haagplantdirect), or unstated. For a nursery that customers can visit, "free to collect at the nursery, or delivered at cost" is a common market pattern.

### Gaps
- No shop publishes average claim-handling times or approval rates.
- Directplant, Tuinplant.nl and Rhododendronwal do not say who pays for replacement shipping.

## 6. Returns: right of withdrawal (herroepingsrecht) for live plants; damaged-on-arrival procedure

### Takeaway
There is no common market practice. Many shops exclude all live plants from the 14-day withdrawal right as "bederfelijk" (perishable): Directplant, Tuinplant.nl, Plantcentraal, Haagplantenwinkel, Bomenbezorgd (but with a goodwill return) and Gras & Haag. Others exclude only bare-root stock (Haag-planten.nl, Bomenenzo, Garmundo). Still others offer 14-day withdrawal on plants with return or pickup costs paid by the customer (Rhododendronwal, Aalst, Haagplantdirect, Haagaanplanten if not yet planted). Damage reports must be made at delivery, within 48 hours, within 3 days or within 14 days, always with photos.

- A nursery near Otterlo selling heavy root-ball rhododendrons might borrow Bomenbezorgd's door-refusal-plus-48-hour-photo model, together with Tuinplantenwinkel's rule that the shop arranges the return transport itself ("nooit zelf planten … terugsturen").
- Legal note (background knowledge, **not verified in this session**): since 1 January 2022, Dutch consumer law presumes a defect existed at delivery if it appears within 1 year, up from the earlier 6 months. Esveld's terms still say "6 maanden". The report writer should verify this before relying on it.

### Gaps
- Bomenbezorgd's FAQ (48 h) and its returns page ("tot 14 dagen") give two windows for damage reports, and the relationship between them is not spelled out.
- Directplant's damaged-on-arrival procedure was not found on the pages read.

## 7. Paid extra guarantees, or guarantees tied to buying soil or fertiliser

### Takeaway
No shop sells a stand-alone paid "garantie-pakket". Two hedge sellers in Borne **tie the guarantee to a soil product**. At Haagplantdirect there is no guarantee without Legro Aanplantgrond. At Gardline an extra month at 10% is earned by buying DCM Vivimus. Planting services (Bomenbezorgd, Haagaanplanten, Haagplantdirect) are paid add-ons that carry or require the guarantee, and Bomenbezorgd also requires stakes and a tree tie for trees.

### Cited Findings
- Haagplantdirect: "Voor in aanmerking komende pot- en kluitplanten bieden wij 3 maanden aangroeigarantie, mits bij de aanplant Legro Aanplantgrond wordt gebruikt en aan de overige garantievoorwaarden wordt voldaan … Zonder Legro Aanplantgrond geldt geen aangroeigarantie." Homepage: "Bij onze aanplantservice gebruiken we standaard Legro Aanplantgrond." [3 months only if Legro planting soil is used; without it, no guarantee.] — [Haagplantdirect aangroeigarantie](https://haagplantdirect.nl/aangroeigarantie/), [home](https://haagplantdirect.nl/)
- Gardline: "Wil jij een extra maand aangroeigarantie? Koop de DCM Vivimus Bodemverbeteraar samen met de aankoop van jouw nieuwe haag … en ontvang een extra maand aangroeigarantie! Als een bij ons gekochte plant de 4e maand na levering niet overleeft, krijg je een terugbetaling van 10%. Let op: 1 zak DCM Vivimus Bodemverbeteraar 60L is goed voor een haag van 6 meter breed … Dan heb je 2 zakken … nodig om in aanmerking te komen" [Buy DCM Vivimus soil improver with your hedge and get an extra month: 10% refund if the plant dies in month 4; 1 × 60 L bag per 6 m of hedge required.] — [Gardline aangroeigarantie](https://www.gardline.nl/aangroeigarantie-op-onze-planten)
- Haagaanplanten: guarantee only with their planting (prices "inclusief de aanplantservice, bemeste potgrond en btw"), and the conditions include "Gebruik van onze goede kwaliteit potgrond als de tuingrond in een slechte staat verkeert." — [Haagaanplanten home](https://www.haagaanplanten.nl/), [garantie](https://www.haagaanplanten.nl/garantie-en-herroeping)
- Bomenbezorgd: the guarantee is the same with or without planting service, but "We raden aan om in combinatie met de aanplantservice ook het aanplantpakket te bestellen. Dit pakket bevat … boompalen, boombanden, boomspijkers en aanplantgrond." Not using stakes or the tie voids the tree guarantee. Failed planting-service visits: "brengen we 50% van de kosten van de aanplantservice alsnog in rekening." — [Bomenbezorgd FAQ](https://bomenbezorgd.nl/veelgestelde-vragen), [aanplantservice](https://bomenbezorgd.nl/aanplantservice)
- Haagplantenwinkel: proof of "juiste en voldoende aanplantgrond, zoals potgrond, aanplantgrond of tuinturf" is required. Any brand is accepted, not necessarily bought from the shop. — [Haagplantenwinkel retourbeleid](https://haagplantenwinkel.nl/retourbeleid/)

### Inferences
- Tying the guarantee to a soil product works both as an upsell and as a way to remove the "bad soil" excuse. For rhododendrons, which need acid, humus-rich, well-drained soil, requiring rhododendron or ericaceous planting soil (own or a named brand) would follow the same logic.

### Gaps
- No paid "extended guarantee" or insurance-style product was found at any shop.

### Inferences
- Because claim experiences are rarely visible publicly, the **wording of the policy itself** is what prospective buyers compare. Proactive aftercare e-mails (as Directplant's reviewer describes) and an included planting schedule (Garmundo) are mentioned positively and probably reduce failed plantings, and so claims.

### Gaps
- Kiyoh (Bomenbezorgd and others) and most Trustpilot pages could not be read (JavaScript rendering or 403). Google Reviews was not accessible.
- No verified first-hand account of a guarantee claim being approved or rejected was found for any shop covered.
